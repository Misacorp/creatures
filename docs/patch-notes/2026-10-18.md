# Patch notes, 18 October

## New

- **Every evolving creature can grow two ways.** At level 15 you pick one of two forms, side by side, and the choice is for good:
  - Sproutle becomes Thornbuck or Sunfawn.
  - Zipmouse becomes Voltaro or Ohmouse.
  - Splashet becomes Tidewing or Geyserpup.
  - Cindle becomes Pyrelynx or Wickat.
  - Mistnewt becomes Fogwyrm or Drizzlewt.
  - Ashmole becomes Magmole or Kilnmole.
  - Stormray becomes Tempestray or Halcyray.
  - Mossback becomes Ancientshell or Bloomback.

  Each new form plays a new role for its element and brings its own finale ability.

- **Keep a duplicate.** When you catch a second creature of a line that grows two ways, you can keep it and raise it the other way.
- **Three new kinds of weather.**
  - Kind Rain mends every creature a little at the start of each round, and the Tidepools and the Verdant Hollow give more parts.
  - Morning Haze makes the first creature of each row miss its first turn, and trips take a bit longer.
  - From Boss Rank 6, Bramble Season throws part of the first reaction each round back at the creature that set it off.
- **Gear sets.** Ten sets of three pieces (a Hat, a Scarf and a Charm) start dropping at item level 12. Wear two pieces of one set for a bonus, and all three for a bigger one. Set pieces have their own gold-trimmed look, and you can craft them from Crafting level 6.
- **Gear that cares where you stand.** New lines reward a creature's place in its row. Edge-walker likes the ends of a row, Heart of the row likes the middle, and Lantern bearer helps the creatures beside it.
- **Lucky gear and gear that reacts.** Rare and Epic gear can roll a line that sometimes fires on its own, such as Echo (the hit lands again) and Sidestep (dodge a sweep). Other lines react to a moment in the fight, such as Payback after taking a hit for a friend and Stubborn, which keeps a creature standing at 1 HP once a fight. You see each one happen during the fight.
- **Luck outside fights.** One new line gives a dismantle a chance to refund the full crafting cost. Another gives a duplicate's talent a chance to roll twice, and you keep the better one.

## Quality of life

- **Flip through creatures and items.** On a creature's page or an item's card, swipe left or right, or use the arrows, to see the next one without going back to the list.
- **Cleaner weather numbers.** The forecast shows whole percents from 10% up and halves below, and the weather does exactly what it says.
- **One special line per item.** An item carries at most one position, chance or reaction line, so your choices stay easy to read.
