# Patch notes, 16 October

## New

- **Bosses have new moves.** Sweeps, shells, slams, lances and pincers keep a row on its toes. Charged moves are announced a round ahead, so you can get ready.
- **Stronger bosses learn extra moves.** The higher the Boss Rank, the more tricks a boss has, and some punish careless rows.
- **Raid weeks have a new shape.** Friday and Saturday drive the boss down until it retreats. Sunday is all or nothing, and if time runs out the boss turns Enraged and overtime begins.
- **A won finale pays everyone.** You get a gear piece (better at higher ranks, already upgraded) or a new blueprint.
- **Dungeon landmarks.** Every 7th depth has a named boss with a move all its own. Room bosses turn Enraged when time runs out.

## Buffs

- **Guards wear armor** and step in for friends, even against reflected hits. With two Guards nearby, a roll decides which one takes it. Guards also cover their neighbours.
- **Supports have element touches.** Ember boosts harder, Spark adds reaction damage, Tide and Grove heal.
- **A daily allowance.** Open the game and get 25 Bait. A busy day pays a bonus on top.
- **An easier first raid.** This week's raid has been re-tuned, so a new server's first boss should feel very doable.

## Nerfs

- **Big hits stay big.** Boss hits are no longer capped, so protect your carry.
