## Levers changed (one at a time, with the reason)

1. Training cost `10 + 5 x L` -> `3 + 2 x L` (`RULES.training`). A casual earns about 110 Bait a week (about 440 in four weeks); level 15 cost 665 Bait even with every coin on one creature. It now costs 252. Casual top level at week 4: 4 -> 15 (median; most casuals sit at the cap and wait for the 20 Essence to evolve around week 4-5).
2. Simulator training policy (player model, `packages/sim/src/policy.ts`). It trained the weakest of the 3 strongest trainable creatures, so every freshly caught level-1 rare took over the Bait and the top level stayed at 4-5. People push one creature to its evolution instead. It now trains the furthest-along creature that still has to reach its evolution level and only spreads evenly when none of the top 3 is working towards one. Without this the target needed training about 5x cheaper (`2 + L`), which would cap dailies at level 30 by week 8.
3. Boss HP exponent 0.9 -> 1.2 (`hpExponent`). Phase 1 damage was 30% / 38% / 42% of the boss at 5 / 10 / 15 players because bigger servers earn more Bait per head (leet points table) and cover more elements. At 1.2 it is 35% / 34% / 34%. 15-player finale win: 99% -> 57%.
4. Boss HP per average level 7% -> 12% (`hpPerLevelBp`). A week-4 roster lost 27% of the boss per phase and a week-8 roster 33%; now 31% and 32%.
5. Phase thresholds 20% / 45% -> 18% / 54%. Damage per phase now clusters around 31% (spread about 13%): Phase 1 at 18% clears for 100% of typical groups, Phase 2 at 54% for 76%.
6. Reactions x2.5 -> x4.5, payoff bonus x1.25 -> x1.1 (all four). Setup credit was stuck at 10-13% because only about a quarter of hits can react; x4.0 gave 14.9%, x4.5 gives 16.6%. The smaller payoff bonus keeps Cindle and the other payoffs from running away from their rarity (Cindle 1.84x -> 1.41x). A reaction is now about half of all damage.
7. Boss base HP, tuned per boss on the average of six seeds: Tidal Colossus 4724, Cinder Wyrm 2710, Thornmother 4296, Storm Sovereign 4380 (were 8500, 6000, 8500, 8500). Win rates by boss 49% / 51% / 47% / 50%.
8. Creature stats: Mistnewt and Fogwyrm Attack 20 -> 28 / 28 -> 38 (growth 1 -> 2 / 2 -> 3): the guard was picked 0.2% of the time, only by weak early players, which made its average contribution 0.37x of its rarity; now picked 4% and 0.9x. Gaiadrake Attack 64 -> 56 and Pyrelynx Attack growth 4 -> 3 (both payoffs were above 1.6x). Sproutle and Thornbuck Attack +2 to keep the starter guard above 0.6x.

Not changed: leet points table and cap, 7 Bait per point, hard pity at 10 raids, 70/30 Essence split, schedule, the target values, soft-pity and jackpot numbers.

## Remaining failures

Both are the pity targets; they cannot both be met with the current rule.

- Jackpot rate per finale win 18.9% (needs 6-15%) and players reaching hard pity 66.4% (needs <= 15%). The pity counter advances on every raid a player takes part in, including lost finales (the worker does this too), but a jackpot is only rolled on a win. At a finale win rate of about 50%, a player reaches 10 raids after about 5 rolls, so most hit hard pity, and a guaranteed jackpot every 10 raids is already 20% of their wins. With losses counting, no base chance or soft-pity curve satisfies both targets unless finales are won about two times in three, which breaks the finale target. The rate target passed before only because finales were too easy (68% wins).
- Owner decision: count only finale wins (the jackpot rolls) toward pity. Measured with `PITY_COUNTS_LOSSES = false`: jackpot rate 11.0% (passes), hard pity 17.8% (just over). Starting soft pity at raid 5 instead of 6 gives about 7% hard pity and 12% rate in a separate Monte Carlo of the same model. This changes the worker's pity rule (`rollJackpots` in `raid-resolve.ts`), so it is left for a decision.
