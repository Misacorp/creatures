# Threads: open lore seeds

Status: 2026-10-13. Open questions and hinted places, characters and traditions that the shipped content mentions but does not explain. New content can answer a thread. When it does, mark the thread **answered** here, with the PR and the new canon.

Read `README.md` for the tags. Every thread names its source. Ideas are `(compendium detail, not shipped)` and are free to change. Do not treat an idea as canon until it ships.

**How to use this file:** pick a thread that fits your work, and answer it in a small way. Do not answer all of them at once. A thread that stays open is a good thing: it gives the next writer something to find.

## Characters

### T01. The night singer

- **Seed:** "Something sings across the water that makes the boss forget its counter-move for a few breaths. Then it stops, and all is quiet." `(content: journal.json reef-night-singer)`
- **Open:** the singer is never named. Is it a creature we have not met, or one of the Reef's known creatures?
- **Ideas:** Aurorix is the Reef's deep creature and its Aurora Surge purges the adaptation `(content: finale-abilities.json)`. This would be a good fit `(inferred)`. Or a new Reef creature that only sings at night.
- **Status:** open.

### T02. The watcher's face

- **Seed:** "Far above the thunder, a ribbon of light hangs over the team... The crowd has never seen its face." `(content: journal.json storm-aurora-watcher)`
- **Open:** why is its face hidden? Is the face a reveal for a Festival, or for a deep-area star?
- **Ideas:** keep the face off-screen in art, and let the Festival's last night be the only time it is seen `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. The art rule "never show the face" is a hook for a future reveal.
- **Status:** open.

### T03. The guests

- **Seed:** three guests, named only by role and species: Guest Warden (Sproutle), Guest Tidecaller (Splashet), Guest Striker (Stormray). `(content: guests.json)`
- **Open:** who are the guests, where do they come from, and why do they visit? No description is shipped.
- **Ideas:** each guest shares a species with a creature the explorer can already meet, so the guests may be visiting cousins from the same lines `(inferred)`. A guest might also be a visitor from another valley (see the server-as-valley idea in `world.md`) `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T04. The Solmane and the dark

- **Seed:** Solmane "is a little afraid of the dark, but denies it" `(content: quirks.json)`, and it is found in the deepest Ember Caves, where "a mane of light walks first" `(content: journal.json ember-lion-light)`.
- **Open:** why does a creature of light live in the deep dark? Is the light its own, or does it bring the light with it?
- **Ideas:** the Solmane walks into the dark so that others can see `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. This also fits the lantern motif.
- **Status:** open.

### T05. The gaiadrake's walk

- **Seed:** Gaiadrake's deep area is both the Verdant Hollow and the Old Crossroads `(content: destinations.json)`. "Something huge sleeps in the deeper area" `(content: glossary.json, former destination text)`.
- **Open:** why does a mountain-sized drake show up in two places? Does it walk between them?
- **Ideas:** it is the Crossroads' traveller, carrying the Hollow's forests and rivers on its back. The "four roads" might be its path `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T06. The storm king and the storm ray

- **Seed:** the Storm Sovereign is "a crowned king of thunderclouds" `(content: bosses-lore.json)`, and the Tempestray "stirs up whole lightning storms" `(content: species-lore.json)`.
- **Open:** are they kin? Did the Tempestray grow from the Sovereign's storm, or is it his subject?
- **Ideas:** the Tempestray is the Sovereign's herald `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. Keep them as kin, not the same creature.
- **Status:** open.

### T07. The hedges

- **Seed:** the Bramble Matron is "the old hedge that all the little hedges follow" `(content: dungeon-foes.json)`. The Bramble Hound is "a hedge that learned to run" `(content)`. The Thornmother is "the old queen of thorns" `(content: journal.json verdant-both-ends)`.
- **Open:** is the Matron the Thornmother's family, or a separate queen? Who taught the hedges to run?
- **Ideas:** a family of hedges, led by the Thornmother, with the Matron as her eldest in the dungeon `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T08. The Tidal Colossus's rest

- **Seed:** the Colossus is "a mountain of sea water and barnacled stone that rises from the deep" `(content: bosses-lore.json)`. The Tidepools are "warm shallows" `(content: destinations.json)`.
- **Open:** where does it rest between weeks? Is it near the Tidepools or the Moonlit Reef?
- **Ideas:** it sleeps beyond the Tidepools' last pool. The deepest Reef edge "lights up like a wall" `(content: journal.json reef-glowing-wall)`, which could be the Colossus's door `(inferred)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T09. The Cinder Wyrm's home

- **Seed:** the Cinder Wyrm is "black stone and living magma" `(content: bosses-lore.json)`. The Ember Caves are "a warm tunnel of glowing rock where something is always humming" `(content: destinations.json)`.
- **Open:** the content does not say where the Wyrm lives. The Caves are the obvious place `(inferred)`.
- **Ideas:** the something that hums is the Wyrm `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

## Places and hooks

### T10. The sunken door

- **Seed:** the Drowned Warden "guards a sunken door nobody has opened. It opens its own gates for you instead." `(content: dungeon-foes.json)`
- **Open:** what is behind the door? Why does the warden open the gates?
- **Ideas:** the door leads to a deeper dungeon. A landmark depth might open onto it `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T11. The ancient vault and the old symbol

- **Seed:** "A door older than any road. What is behind it is worth a great deal, or nothing." `(content: push-on-goals.json ancient-vault)`. "A sealed chest with an old symbol on the lid. Nobody knows what is inside." `(content: push-on-goals.json lost-cache)`
- **Open:** what is the old symbol? Does it match a sign at the Old Crossroads?
- **Ideas:** the symbol is the Crossroads' signpost mark, and the vault is the place the four roads were built to reach `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T12. The four roads

- **Seed:** the Old Crossroads banner has "a signpost with four arrows, each pointing along a different path" and "four paths in four slightly different colours" `(content: destinations.json)`. The Signpost Series journal set has four pages `(content: journal.json)`.
- **Open:** where do the four roads go? Do they lead to the four elemental places?
- **Ideas:** each road leads to one element's place, and the Crossroads is where they meet `(inferred)`. This would explain why the Crossroads is "mixed" `(content)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T13. The silver tide and the lost way

- **Seed:** "By morning it is gone, and nobody remembers the way." `(content: journal.json reef-silver-tide)`. Drift Wisp "forgot the way home" `(content: dungeon-foes.json)`.
- **Open:** how does an explorer find the Reef again? Is there a way that lasts?
- **Ideas:** the Moon Lantern shows the hidden places' doors `(content: supplies.json)`. It is the way home `(inferred)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T14. The Ember Caves' forge

- **Seed:** the Forge Band "hammered flat on a forge that was left running overnight" `(content: gear.json)`. The Slag Tyrant is "king of a cooled forge" `(content: dungeon-foes.json)`.
- **Open:** which forge was left running, and which one has cooled? Is the Tyrant the forge's last keeper?
- **Ideas:** the forge that was left running is the Ember Caves' heart, and the Tyrant is what happens when a forge goes out `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T15. The dungeon's landmarks

- **Seed:** every seventh depth is a landmark with a guaranteed gear drop `(content: glossary.json "landmark")`.
- **Open:** what is at a landmark? Are the landmarks the same place, or a set of places?
- **Ideas:** each landmark is a room that is older than the dungeon, and a glimmer is the thing that is left there `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. See T19.
- **Status:** open.

### T16. The dungeon's seal

- **Seed:** the shipped text says the dungeon is one run a day, with a team of three, and that the day resets at 13:37 `(content: glossary.json "dungeon")`. The design calls it a solo daily mode `(design: daily-dungeon.md)`. Nothing says what the dungeon is.
- **Open:** why does the dungeon run on a daily cycle, and who made it?
- **Ideas:** the dungeon resets every day at 13:37, the server's reset `(content: glossary.json "dungeon")`. The day is the seal's rhythm `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

## Festival and traditions

### T17. The Festival of the tide

- **Seed:** the Festival Lantern Charm is "lit once a season at the Festival of the tide" `(content: gear.json)`.
- **Open:** what is the Festival named for? Is the tide the festival's sponsor, and does every element have a festival?
- **Ideas:** the Festival is the tide's festival, and the Hearth Band is the ember's. Each season has a different sponsor `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T18. The last night

- **Seed:** "On the last night the whole meadow goes quiet for a moment, then fireworks open the sky and everyone remembers why they came." `(content: journal.json fest-last-night)`
- **Open:** what is the last night? Does it mark the end of the season `(inferred)`?
- **Ideas:** the last night is the Festival's finale, and the Festival creature is the thing the explorer takes home `(content: glossary.json "festival")`. Use the quiet moment as the scene for a reveal.
- **Status:** open.

### T19. The festival boss

- **Seed:** "The festival boss is always a little easier than the last one. Everybody is in too good a mood to be afraid of it." `(content: journal.json fest-bunting)`
- **Open:** is there a festival boss of its own? The content says the Festival fights a regular boss three Ranks lower `(content: glossary.json "festival")`. The page says "the festival boss", which could mean either.
- **Ideas:** a named festival boss, friendly and small, who appears only in Festival weeks `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. Keep it out of `bosses.json` until it is designed.
- **Status:** open.

### T20. The Lanternfin's winter

- **Seed:** the Lanternfin is "seen only drifting through festival nights" `(content: creatures.json)`, and "falls asleep long before the party ends" `(content: quirks.json)`.
- **Open:** where do Lanternfin and Hearthshell go between festivals?
- **Ideas:** the Lanternfin sleeps in the deep water of the Tidepools until the next festival `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T21. The fire that never goes out

- **Seed:** "The fire never goes out." `(content: journal.json fest-hearth-ward)`. The Hearthshell "shows up when a festival begins" `(content: species-lore.json)`. The Festival Hearth Band is given "to everyone who stood by the Festival hearth" `(content: gear.json)`.
- **Open:** where is the Festival hearth? Is it the same fire the Hearthshell carries?
- **Ideas:** the Festival hearth is the first fire, and each Hearthshell carries a spark of it `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open.

### T22. The festival's warm hands

- **Seed:** the Ashmole's burrow: "a team that shares a burrow glows with it for a few rounds, and hits a little harder while it lasts" `(content: journal.json ember-ash-burrow)`. The Festival Grounds have Ashmole and Solmane `(content: destinations.json)`.
- **Open:** is the Ashmole burrow a home that becomes a festival place? Is the glow a tradition?
- **Ideas:** the burrow is the first warm place a new explorer visits, and the glow is a tradition of sharing `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. See `README.md`, contradiction 2.
- **Status:** open.

## Mechanics hinted at in the world

### T23. Three coins

- **Seed:** "Three coins buy a better road, and the rest are only for luck." `(content: journal.json road-wishing-well)`
- **Open:** what does "three coins" mean? The content has no three-part spend that matches. It may be a three-slot unlock, three Bait, or three trips `(inferred)`.
- **Ideas:** a well that gives back a little of what you drop in, which could be a small Bait return on three trips `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. Decide the mechanic first, then the page.
- **Status:** open.

### T24. Do bosses remember?

- **Seed:** "The big ones come back with a new trick and a long memory." `(content: journal.json any-boss-adapts)`. The adaptation is per fight `(content: adaptations.json)`.
- **Open:** does the memory last across weeks, or only within a fight? A boss that remembers last week's fight would be a new mechanic.
- **Ideas:** a memory that carries from one week to the next, as a stronger Rank, not a new trick `(inferred; content: glossary.json "boss-rank")`.
- **Status:** open.

### T25. The glimmer

- **Seed:** "A rare part... found by dismantling Epic gear, at dungeon landmarks, and rarely on expeditions." `(content: glossary.json "part-glimmer")`. No glimmer item exists in the data (`README.md`, contradiction 12).
- **Open:** what is a glimmer? Where does the bright splinter come from?
- **Ideas:** a glimmer is the last of an Epic piece's power, left behind when the piece is taken apart `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. The landmarks are where epic pieces were first made.
- **Status:** open.

### T26. Who writes the blueprints?

- **Seed:** blueprints "teach you to craft one kind of item", and the welcome kit gives one `(content: glossary.json "blueprint")`. The Lost Pages set has three pages `(content: journal.json)`.
- **Open:** who wrote the crafting notes? Are the Lost Pages the same hand?
- **Ideas:** the neat, dry hand that writes blueprints is the Workshop's first teacher `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. See `items.md`.
- **Status:** open.

### T29. The wheel never stops

- **Seed:** "The wheel has never once stopped." `(content: journal.json any-the-wheel)`.
- **Open:** who turns the wheel? Does the wheel have a centre?

### T30. The garden that others harvest

- **Seed:** "A turtle with a garden on its back plants a seed in whatever it meets. Others come along later and harvest it." `(content: journal.json verdant-quiet-garden)`
- **Open:** is there a trade in gardens? Do explorers sell what the Mossback plants?
- **Ideas:** the Mossback's seeds are a loose network, where each explorer's trip harvests another's seed `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. It is a quiet, communal idea that fits the Overgrown mark.
- **Status:** open.

### T31. The noise of the boss

- **Seed:** the Thornmother's "Bramble Bind snares the creatures at both ends of each row" `(content: bosses-lore.json)`. The Lightning Rod "always picks out the hardest hitter" `(content)`. Each boss has one clear way of acting, and each is big in its valley `(inferred)`.
- **Open:** what do the bosses want? The content says what they do, not why.
- **Ideas:** the bosses go for the strongest and the loudest, because that is what they were made to keep watch over `(compendium detail, not shipped)`. Keep their motives gentle; they are not villains.
- **Status:** open.

### T32. The things the bog keeps

- **Seed:** "The bog keeps old things: its trip place turns up old finds." `(design: speed-and-dodge.md section 5.6)`. The Ancient vault is "a door older than any road" `(content: push-on-goals.json ancient-vault)`, and the Sunken Fen banner (draft) has a half-sunk stone arch with a lantern under it.
- **Open:** what has the fen swallowed, and who put it there? Is the half-sunk arch the vault's door, seen from the other side? Does the same bog hold the sunken door of the Drowned Warden (T10)?
- **Ideas:** the fen is where the world keeps what it does not want to forget (see motif 14, "Keeping", in `world.md`). Old Bogback sits on top of it all. A page in the Peat Notes set could say what the first find was `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open (seeded 2026-10-19, design not shipped).

- **Open:** if there is a fifth element, are there more? What does an explorer need to find to find a sixth? Is the loop itself, as the old journal says, "never once stopped", or did it only look that way from inside?
- **Ideas:** a future element could sit between two old ones the way Mire sits between Tide and Ember. Keep the Mire rule (an element can be strong against one element and weak to another) as the pattern. Do not answer this yet; it is a good thread to leave open `(compendium detail, not shipped)`.
- **Status:** open (seeded 2026-10-19).
