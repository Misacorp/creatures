# The raid as defending the valley

Status: **direction accepted** (Misa, 2026-10-29; `docs/design/decisions.md`, "The raid is defending the valley"). The raid's rules belong to combat v2 (`docs/design/combat/`, branch `combat-v2-bosses`); this doc owns the valley side.

## The direction

The raid is not a show in an arena. Each week a threat rises against the valley and the creatures go out to meet it far from home, before it reaches the valley (Misa, round 5, Q14: "The raid is proactive defense and happens in far away places"): a dangerous trial, framed and drawn as one. Each boss element has its own battleground; a gauntlet of trials on the way is a future idea (on `combat-v2-bosses` the word "gauntlet" is only the combat lab's one-row test).

## In the valley

- **V-DEF-1** The Watchtower at the east gate is the raid's place in the valley: where the defenders muster and set out, and where the week's threat and its rule cards are shown from Monday. It holds no post (V-DEF-4); Misa is \"still skeptical about the watchtower concept in general\", so its name and role are revisited with the valley's art. When this week's threat is sighted, its beacon is lit and the sky at the valley's edge takes the threat's colour.
- **V-DEF-2** Muster at the gate: each player holds a column of the line (combat v2: one column per player, the boss sets the rows).
- **V-DEF-3** The weekend (combat v2's R14): three stages against one boss whose HP carries over; creatures do not heal between stages and a fallen one is out for the weekend; swaps at each break. Defenders leave their posts for the weekend; a stand-in may hold the seat (V-POST-7).
- **V-DEF-4** No sentry post (Misa, round 5, Q15). A post must give something all week, and the raid's preparation is the whole week: everything a player needs to plan for the raid (the boss, its rule cards) is known to everyone from Monday, without a post. If a real week-long use for a Watchtower post turns up later, it can come back; it is not forced in.
- **V-DEF-5** The aftermath tells the defence as a story in the return moment: who held, who fell, what the valley earned.
