# Scouts: expeditions as standing posts

Status: **proposed** (written in full 2026-10-30, for the panel). Accepted by Misa so far: standing scouts instead of timed trips, the player choosing "which areas are being explored" (round 1, Q2); kit instead of supplies (panel, 2026-10-30); push on with a trade-off (V-RET-3). Builds on `posts.md` (seats, fit, mastery, kit, away steps, the one lazy pass) and `return.md`.

## The idea

A scout is posted to one of the destinations and roams it all the time. Finds turn up as it goes and pile up at its post; the return walk shows it carrying them home. Where a scout stands is the choice: each place has its own residents, its own finds and its own journal pages, and each creature has its own fit there. Scouts are the valley's source of new creatures, Acorns, scrap and shards, blueprints and lore, so they feed the Forge, the commons and the collection.

## Rules

- **V-SCOUT-1** Seats: each player has scout seats at the Signpost (example: 2 at its level 1, one more a level, `posts.md`). A seat is posted to one destination; moving it is free. Several of a player's scouts can share a destination.
- **V-SCOUT-2** Finds per hour. A scout rolls finds for each effective hour (the away steps apply, V-POST-10), on the server at the moment the valley is read, with live randomness (F-8). Finds are the destination's own: Acorns, scrap, shards of the place's element, now and then a creature that lives there, a gear piece, a blueprint, glimmer, a journal page and, very rarely, the place's keystone (`commons.md`). Example rates are below.
- **V-SCOUT-3** New creatures arrive at the Paddock gate. A new species is kept, celebrated in the return walk (V-RET-2) and joins the roster at once. A duplicate waits at the gate as a bubble, with today's choices (teach, fuse, release, keep); the gate holds a generous number and never turns one away.
- **V-SCOUT-4** The store holds about three days of finds (V-POST-5); when full, the scout keeps exploring (and growing) but stops picking up, and its bubble says "pack full". Nothing is lost.
- **V-SCOUT-5** Kit (V-POST-11): a scout's seat may hold one kit item, bought once with Acorns and kept; switching between kit a player owns is free. A **lure** draws creatures (more often, and leaning toward a species the player lacks, with a hint of which); a **lantern** draws rarer finds (gear, blueprints, pages, glimmer); a **satchel** brings more Acorns and scrap. One at a time: a real trade.
- **V-SCOUT-6** Push on: now and then a scout finds a way into the destination's deeper area, and the return walk can offer it as its one decision. Going deeper is a trade-off, never a free yes: rarer finds and the deeper area's own residents, but fewer Acorns and scrap while there. Coming back up is free at any time. A scout must be at least **Expert** at scouting to be offered the way deeper (V-POST-12).
- **V-SCOUT-7** Mastery in scouting (V-POST-12): Skilled, Expert and Master raise a scout's finds a little; Expert opens push on; a **Master Scout** also notices a little more at any other post it works at (example: a Master Scout at the Forge sometimes turns up a blueprint mark).
- **V-SCOUT-8** Night and festival places keep their rules: the Moonlit Reef by night, the Festival Grounds in festival weeks. A scout there waits at camp while the place is closed and earns XP like a resting creature.
- **V-SCOUT-9** The week's twist (today's weekly special) still lands on one destination and changes what it gives for the week, shown on the Signpost from Monday.

## Example rates per scout (the economy lab sets the real ones)

Today's trips pay in "ticks" of about an hour (`RULES.expeditions.tick`). A scout works every hour of the day, about twice as many hours as a daily player's trips today, so most rates are lower per hour.

| Find                               | Per effective hour  | Per day (24 h, before fit, mastery and kit)   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Acorns                             | 3                   | 72                                            |
| Scrap                              | 1 (50% chance of 2) | about 36                                      |
| Shard of the place's element       | 15% chance          | about 3.6                                     |
| A creature                         | 2% chance           | about 0.5                                     |
| A gear piece (the place's element) | 2% chance           | about 0.5                                     |
| A blueprint                        | 0.3% chance         | one every two weeks or so                     |
| Glimmer                            | 0.2% chance         | one every three weeks or so                   |
| A journal page                     | 0.4% chance         | one every ten days or so                      |
| The place's keystone               | very rare           | one per server every few weeks (`commons.md`) |

Kit tilts (examples): a lure x2 creature chance and a lean toward a missing species, x0.7 Acorns; a lantern x1.5 gear, blueprints, glimmer and pages, x0.7 scrap; a satchel x1.4 Acorns and scrap. Fit adds 25% to everything; mastery up to 15%.

With two scouts, a daily player brings home about 150 Acorns, 70 scrap, 7 shards and a creature a day: the Acorn budget in `posts.md`, and enough scrap for two busy anvils (`forge.md`).

## What it replaces

Expeditions' timed trips, trip lengths, slots, recall, the claim, explorer ranks (now scouting mastery), supplies packed per trip (now kit) and the push-on goal at a trip's end (`scrap-list.md`). The destinations, their residents, deeper areas, stars and weekly twists stay.
