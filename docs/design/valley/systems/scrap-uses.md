# Scrap uses: where the late scrap goes

Status: **deferred** (Misa, 2026-11-01: "Let's just not focus on the scrap problem after week 13 now and agree to tackle it later"; the lab pretends an idol takes the surplus meanwhile). Proposed by the lead, 2026-11-01, from Misa's answer to Q49 (round 20): no tempering; "Can we introduce more places to use scrap? Week 13 is far away and I think we could get some more uses for it into the trader, market or new upcoming game systems by the e.g., buy exotic wood for furniture crafting, fund a massive communal project, etc." To be modelled in the economy lab against T-7b, then put to the panel and to Misa where it is a matter of feel.

## The problem in numbers

From about week 13 a daily player's scrap comes in about 500 a week faster than it goes out (scouts about 1.9k a week; pieces about 680, project gifts about 660, the visitor about 560). Gifts are small because a project only takes what it still needs, and only one project per place is open. The pile has no use, so it is "dead" (T-7b).

## The proposal: three uses, each arriving with the problem

1. **A great work** (Misa's "massive communal project"). One server-wide project, separate from the places' levels, that opens once the server's scrap starts piling (example: week 6 to 8) and is paid mostly in scrap. It is built in stages over months, each stage a visible change to the valley everyone sees (example: a bridge across the river, stone by stone, to an island that later holds new content), with the plaque and the keepsake of V-COM-2 and V-COM-3. It never fails or expires (V-COM-4), and its size scales with the server's active players (V-COM-5), so a small server builds it more slowly, not never. It is the valley's long goal between raids.
2. **The visitor takes scrap for more than rerolls** (V-VIS-5). One scrap-priced line in his weekly stock, rotating: a bundle of one element's shards at a poor rate (the scarce resource for the plentiful one, which also eases the Forge's waits for a shard), a lure, a page of a collection. Per player and fixed for the visit, as the rest of his stock.
3. **The Market's later levels cost scrap** (V-FORGE-11, V-COM-15). Its first level stays small (Acorns and scrap, finished in the first week or two); levels 2 and up, which improve the broker's rate, take a larger scrap share than other places' projects.

## Not now

- **Crafting pastimes that buy materials** (Misa's example: exotic wood for furniture). A good sink, but it needs a new system and art, and furniture leans toward housing, which is parked (round 16, Q42). If it comes, furniture for the valley's places first (benches at the bonfire, a sign at the stall), not a player's house.
- **Tempering** (turned down, Q49) and **fewer scrap from scouts** (not taken for now).

## For the lab

Model the three, each behind a switch, with and without, against T-7b and the other targets (T-4, T-7, the visitor's rerolls, gifts to the places' projects, which must not be starved by the great work). Report when each starts to take scrap and how much a week by player profile; the original targets stay unchanged.
