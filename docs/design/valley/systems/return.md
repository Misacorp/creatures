# The return moment: "while you were away"

Status: **proposed** (written in full 2026-10-30, for the panel). Evidence: `docs/research/idle-job-posts.md` ("The short answer: fun by doing nothing"). Builds on Misa's answers: posts with no timers (round 1, Q2), gentle away steps (round 6, Q18), resting creatures' small moments (V-BON-9), the raid as defence (G-5).

## The idea

The reward for idling is coming back. Opening the game is walking into your valley as it wakes up: the creatures who did something step forward one by one, the best find of the day pops out where it was found, friends' work on the server's places shows, and then you get at most one or two real choices. Then it gets out of the way. It replaces today's Home board and its "While you were away" card (`scrap-list.md`).

## Rules

- **V-RET-1** A story, not a sum. One line per notable event, told as who, what and where ("Mossback found a pearl at the Tidepools", "Pip became a Master Smith"). Routine output is one line of count badges ("+212 Acorns, +18 scrap, 3 pieces from the Forge"). At most about five story lines.
- **V-RET-2** One surprise, highlighted: the rarest thing that happened (a new species, a keystone, a mastery, a rare piece), shown as a moment in the world: the camera eases to the place and the find pops out. Never more than one.
- **V-RET-3** At most two decisions, and only real ones, taken from this list in order: a new creature at the Paddock gate (keep, teach or release, as today's catch choice); a hinted visitor a lure could draw (V-POST-11); a scout's way deeper (push on, V-SCOUT-6); a Master "ready to learn something new" (V-POST-12); a finished Forge order (set the next). Anything else waits as a bubble at its place, unasked.
- **V-RET-4** Coming home collects everything. Opening the game brings home all that posts gathered (no collect button anywhere, F-1); the story only tells it. A decision not taken waits as a bubble at its place for as long as it takes; nothing expires; dismissing the story loses nothing.
- **V-RET-5** The server's part: which place grew and who built it ("Sam and 3 others raised the Forge to level 3"), a friend's creature that helped yours, who is online right now (their creatures around the bonfire, V-BON-4). Never who did not help (F-6).
- **V-RET-6** It fits one phone screen and about ten seconds; one tap skips to the valley. A longer absence groups more, it does not grow longer.
- **V-RET-7** No loss meter (F-1, V-POST-10). The story never says what the away steps cost, never shows a reduced rate, never says "you missed". After a long absence it opens with a welcome line ("The valley kept the fire going while you were away") and tells the biggest things that happened.
- **V-RET-8** The week's beats join the story on their day: on Monday, the threat sighted far away and its rule cards (V-DEF-4); on Wednesday, the fair's results; on the first visit after the raid, the defence told as a story: who held, who fell, what the valley earned (V-DEF-5).
- **V-RET-9** When it plays: on the first open after a few hours away (example: 3 hours). A shorter visit goes straight to the valley, where any new bubble shows at its place.
- **V-RET-10** Built cheaply (F-9): the story is made on read from the same one-pass computation as the posts (V-POST-14), which yields events as well as totals; the server keeps the last story so a reload shows it again without recomputing. Only notable events become rows; routine output is totals.

## Examples

**Sanni, every evening (about 24 hours away).**

1. The valley fades in at dusk. Count badges: +180 Acorns, +14 scrap, 2 Kelp Caps from the Forge.
2. "Mossback found a pearl at the Tidepools." "Sprout napped in the new log bench." "Sam and Aino raised the bonfire to level 2."
3. The surprise: at the Ember Caves, a Cindle steps out of the smoke. A new creature at the Paddock gate.
4. Decision 1: keep the Cindle, teach with it, or release it. Decision 2: the Forge finished its order; set the next.
5. Juho and Sam are online; their creatures sit by the fire.

**Juho, every Sunday (a week away).** A welcome line; badges for the whole week; the three biggest lines ("Pip became a Master Smith", "the Signpost opened a new road", "the Tidal Colossus was sighted at the drowned sea gate"); the surprise; one decision (Pip is ready to learn something new). Everything else waits at its place as a bubble.

**A month away.** The welcome line, the server's biggest changes (places that grew), the player's own biggest find, and no decision forced: the valley is there to walk around.

## To prototype first (roadmap phase 3)

With two posts (a scout and the Forge), the bonfire and one server place, on fixture data: a few hours away, a day, a long weekend, a week. Does it read like a story with a real choice, or like a to-do list?
