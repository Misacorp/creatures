# The return moment: "while you were away"

Status: **idea**. Evidence: `docs/research/idle-job-posts.md` ("The short answer: fun by doing nothing").

## The idea

The reward for idling is coming back. Opening the valley tells a short story of what happened, with one surprise and at most one or two real decisions, then gets out of the way.

## Rules (draft)

- **V-RET-1** A story, not a sum: one line per notable event, told as who, what and where ("Mossback found a pearl at the Tidepools", "Pip taught Sprout Thick Hide", "The server's Forge reached level 3"). Routine output is grouped into one line of count badges.
- **V-RET-2** One surprise is highlighted (the rarest thing that happened), shown as a moment in the world (the item pops out at its post).
- **V-RET-3** At most one or two decisions, real ones: who goes where now, which find to keep, which talent to learn. Never a "collect" button for its own sake: collecting happens by walking past the post or in one tap on the story.
- **V-RET-4** Claims never expire; dismissing the story loses nothing (everything stays at its post).
- **V-RET-5** The server's story is part of it: what friends did for the commons, which place grew, who helped you (never who did not, F-6).
- **V-RET-6** It fits one phone screen; longer absences group more, they do not scroll forever.

## To prototype first (roadmap phase 3)

With two posts (a scout and the Forge) and the bonfire, on fixture data: a short absence (a few hours), a day, a long weekend. Does it read like a story with a real choice, or like a to-do list?
