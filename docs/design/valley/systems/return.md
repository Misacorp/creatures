# The return moment: "while you were away"

Status: **proposed**, revised after the panel (`reviews/2026-10-30-return.md`). Waiting on Misa: Q20 (where numbers live) and Q21 (raid preparation in the walk). Evidence: `docs/research/idle-job-posts.md` ("The short answer: fun by doing nothing"). Builds on Misa's answers: posts with no timers (round 1, Q2), gentle away steps (round 6, Q18), resting creatures' small moments (V-BON-9), the raid as defence far away (G-5).

## The idea

The reward for idling is coming back. Opening the game is a short walk through your valley as it wakes up: the camera visits the few places where something happened, creatures carry home what they gathered, a rare find pops out where it was found, and the walk ends at the one place with a real choice. Then you are in the valley. It replaces today's Home board and its "While you were away" card (`scrap-list.md`).

## Rules

- **V-RET-1** A walk, not a card. The camera visits three or four places. Each story line is a bubble over the creature where it happened, told as who, what and where ("Mossback found a pearl", "Pip became a Master Smith"). At most three lines in all.
- **V-RET-2** A surprise only when something is rare enough (a new species, a keystone, a mastery, a rare piece): the camera eases to it and the find pops out. Never more than one. On a quiet day, a resting creature's small moment closes the walk instead (V-BON-9), so every visit has a small smile and never a bare total.
- **V-RET-3** At most one decision, the one that matters most, ending the walk at its place. Candidates: a hinted visitor a lure could draw (a kit trade-off, V-POST-11); a Master ready to learn something new (V-POST-12); a scout's way deeper, with its trade-off (V-SCOUT-6); raid preparation (Q21). A new species is kept and celebrated, a surprise, not a decision. Everything else (a finished Forge order, a duplicate at the Paddock gate, a full store) waits as a bubble at its place, never asked.
- **V-RET-4** Coming home brings everything home. During the walk the creatures carry their piles home, and the count badges tumble into the purse with a small sound; a tap hurries it, but nothing ever needs a tap (F-1). Between visits, the piles grow visibly at the posts. Nothing expires, and skipping the walk loses nothing.
- **V-RET-5** The server's part, at most one of the three lines: your own part first ("Your hauler helped raise the Forge"); if you had none, the place's new look with no names. Never a list of what was built while you were away, never who did not help (F-6). Who is online shows in the valley itself (V-BON-4), not as a line.
- **V-RET-6** About ten seconds; one tap anywhere skips to the valley. A longer absence groups more; it never grows longer.
- **V-RET-7** No loss meter (F-1, V-POST-10). The walk never says what the away steps cost, never shows a reduced rate, never says "you missed". After a long absence it opens with a welcome line ("The valley kept the fire going") and shows only the biggest things.
- **V-RET-8** The week's beats take a line's place, never add to the lines. Monday: the threat is drawn on the far horizon and the Watchtower's beacon is lit; its rule cards wait at the Watchtower. Wednesday: the fair's result. After the raid: the defence as a story if your creatures fought (who held, who fell, what the valley earned); otherwise one line on what the valley earned.
- How it is built: it plays on the first open after a few hours away (example: 3 hours); a shorter visit goes straight to the valley with its bubbles. The walk is made on read from the posts' one-pass computation (V-POST-14), which yields notable events as well as totals; the server keeps the last walk so a reload replays it without recomputing (F-9).

## Examples

**Sanni, every evening (about 24 hours away).** Dusk falls over the valley. At the Tidepools, Mossback holds up a pearl and trots home with a small sack. At the bonfire, Sprout has fallen asleep on the new log bench. The badges tumble into her purse: +180 Acorns, +14 scrap, 2 Kelp Caps. The walk ends at the Ember Caves, where a hint of orange flickers in the smoke: a Cindle might come if she sets a lure there. A lure or keep the lantern? Then she is in the valley; her friends' creatures sit by the fire.

**Juho, every Sunday (a week away).** "The valley kept the fire going." Pip, with a new Master's badge at the Forge; the Signpost's new road; the Tidal Colossus on the horizon past the sea gate. One choice: Pip is ready to learn something new. Everything else waits at its place.

**A month away.** The welcome line, the places that grew (their new look), the player's own biggest find, and no decision: the valley is there to walk around.

## To prototype first (roadmap phase 3)

With two posts (a scout and the Forge), the bonfire and one server place, on fixture data: a few hours away, a day, a long weekend, a week. Does it read like a story with a real choice, or like a to-do list?
