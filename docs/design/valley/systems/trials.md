# Trials: honours instead of ribbons

Status: **accepted** (Misa, round 15, Q39: "Let's drop it" for the ribbon gates; "The honours system is kind of cool"), after the panel (`reviews/2026-10-30-trials.md`). Replaces today's ribbons (levels 10 and 20 wait on materials).

## Misa's idea

Misa (answering Q36): "I'm circumventing the question by questioning the resource gated ribbons to begin with. We originally added [them] in a vain attempt to limit how fast the top creatures in someone's roster level. They turned out to do nothing on that front. Should we drop them entirely or build another type of 'trial' for the creature to complete? I'm thinking a sort of checklist that encompasses more than one game system. Clear a dungeon, take part in a raid, craft an item at the workshop, spend X amount of time on an expedition (or bring something back) - something along these lines. Harder ribbons can require Undercroft clears, raid wins, rare or epic crafts etc. My thinking is that if these requirements are shown to the player from day one (at least the requirements to the next ribbon) then players can tick off items in that list to be pre-eligible for the ribbon promotion (it could even be a daily event at a certain time in the valley, like graduation)."

The lab agrees ribbons do not hold back the best creatures (they pass their gates at once); they hold back the rest of the roster: a hands-off player had about 13 creatures waiting at a ribbon at week 12, for want of the right shards.

## The idea in one breath

Each creature has a next **trial**: a short checklist of things it does across the valley, shown on its card from day one. Ticks happen as the creature lives its life. When every box is ticked, the creature is ready, and it **graduates** at the valley's daily graduation, told in the return walk.

## As revised after the panel

- **V-TRIAL-1** Levels never stop. Ribbon gates go; a trial is an **honour**: a badge, a title ("Pip, Silver") and a ribbon worn on the creature's sprite. No stat bump or slot, so nothing is a must.
- **V-TRIAL-2** Three trials (Bronze from level 10, Silver from 20, Gold from 30), the same lists for every creature. Each has one **combat feat** and a few boxes from the valley's other systems (Misa, Q39): Bronze, a dungeon clear; Silver, an Undercroft clear (or two or more levels deep, set by balance); Gold, a raid clear. Bronze touches many systems and is easy; Silver and Gold may be rare, because nothing waits on them ("If we don't gate any progress behind ribbons, there shouldn't be an issue of requiring rare combat feats for them"). This replaces the panel's "no fight-only boxes", which assumed a gate.
- **V-TRIAL-3** Only the next trial shows, as pips on the creature's card; the list is a tap away. The valley shows "2 ready to graduate".
- **V-TRIAL-4** Graduation is a daily moment at the bonfire (Misa's idea), worked out lazily: a creature whose boxes are ticked graduates at the next ceremony whether or not you are there, and the return walk tells it, several in one line ("Three of yours graduated at the bonfire").
- **V-TRIAL-5** Craft routes count a creature's own smithing over days, not single pieces, so swapping creatures through anvils gains nothing.
- Today's ribbons become "I was there" mementos at the migration (phase 6). The lab moves ribbons' scrap and shard sink elsewhere.

## Example trials (a sketch)

| Trial             | Checklist (examples)                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bronze (level 10) | **Combat:** clear a dungeon run. **Work:** a day at any post. **Bring something home:** a find as a scout, or a day of smithing. **Make a friend:** a small moment at the bonfire |
| Silver (level 20) | **Combat:** clear the Undercroft (or two or more levels deep). **Craft:** Expert in any work, or a Rare piece made. **Go further:** push on to a deeper area, or a keystone found |
| Gold (level 30)   | **Combat:** clear a raid (its boss killed with the creature in the party). **Masterwork:** Master in any work, or an Epic piece made. **Teacher:** a week at a communal seat      |

## Decided

The six open choices were settled by the panel and Misa: trials, as honours (no gates); one list per tier for every creature; combat feats allowed because nothing waits on them; graduation at the bonfire's daily ceremony without having to be there; the School can host it later if it survives.
