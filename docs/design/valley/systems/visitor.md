# The weekly visitor: a merchant with rerolls

Status: **idea** (Misa, round 16, Q42, 2026-10-30). Research and the panel come next (`docs/research/weekly-visitor.md`, `reviews/2026-10-30-visitor.md`).

## Misa's idea

Misa (Q42: "B is the way to go and we can expand on what options we give players over time. Piling up is alright for the first few weeks and that buys us time to build the sinks."): "the trader could have a limited stock of items for sale and it could introduce the first path of exile type 'orbs' that would let players reroll their gear. Perhaps a Chaos Orb type of item that just randomly rerolls all the stats of an item. Epic crafts are rare in the beginning and that would give players a chance to gamble. How the items are used is another thing. Maybe the trader comes around on Thursday, leaves when the raid starts (I eventually want to move raid phase 1 to start on Saturday, phase 2 on Sunday and the finale Sunday evening), and offers a service: 'give your item to me and I'll reroll the modifiers on it'. The vibe of Xur in destiny 2 is what I'm going for here, so 'trader' is a bit underselling it. A separate trader could actually offer deals for some other type of stuff. I'm afraid of housing right now because it requires a ton of new systems and art."

## The idea in one breath

Once a week a mysterious visitor arrives in the valley, with a small, changing stock and one service: hand over a piece of gear and it rerolls all its modifier lines. It is the valley's first way to improve an item after it is made (`gear.md` V-GEAR-5 kept orbs out "at the start"), and the first big sink for the piles of Acorns, scrap and shards.

## Open choices (for the research, the panel and then Misa)

1. **When:** Thursday until the raid starts (Saturday, if the raid moves). A once-a-week player who opens the game on Sundays would never meet the visitor (T-2, F-1).
2. **The reroll:** a service ("I'll reroll it") or an item to keep (an orb); what it costs (Acorns, scrap, shards, Essence); how many per visit; whether a reroll can make an item worse.
3. **Stock:** what the visitor sells (rare blueprints, kit, a creature lure, a keystone hint, an orb), how limited (per player or for the whole server), and how it changes week to week.
4. **A second trader:** a separate, plainer trader for other deals (the shard exchange's better rates, `later.md`).
5. **Where and how it shows:** a figure in the valley, a line in the return walk ("A hooded stranger is at the crossroads until Saturday"), the stock visible from Monday?
