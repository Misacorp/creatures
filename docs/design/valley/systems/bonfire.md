# The bonfire: roster-wide growth

Status: **proposed** (written in full 2026-10-30, for the panel). Accepted by Misa so far: no daily XP cap, the away steps (round 1, Q3), catch-up toward the server (round 4, Q10), the communal hearth with slight increments (Q11). Origin: Misa, 2026-10-29: "idle creatures can sit around a bonfire".

## The idea

Creatures without a post are not wasted: they gather at the bonfire in the middle of the valley and grow slowly but steadily. With posts, this is the floor that lets a player grow their whole roster (G-2), while fights and posts stay the faster routes. The fire belongs to the server: everyone feeds it, and everyone's creatures grow a little faster as it grows.

## Rules

- **V-BON-1** Every creature a player owns earns XP every hour from where it is: a post (the post rate, `posts.md` V-POST-3) or the bonfire (the bonfire rate). Fights pay on top. No daily cap (Misa's goal); only the rates and the away steps (V-POST-10).
- **V-BON-2** Catch-up within a roster: a creature below its player's roster middle (example: the median level of their top 10) earns faster, at the bonfire and at posts, fading to normal as it nears the middle (example: x2 at 10 or more levels below, falling evenly to x1 at the middle). New catches become useful within days, not weeks.
- **V-BON-3** The bonfire has no seats: every creature without a post sits there. On screen it shows a handful around the fire, with "+N" for the rest; a tap opens them all.
- **V-BON-4** The couch in the house is the social spot (today's lounge): one creature, visible to friends. While its player and a friend are both in the Activity, it earns company XP (today's 1 every 2 minutes); otherwise the bonfire rate. No daily cap, so no Rested mark.
- **V-BON-5** Levels are not the main reason to pick a creature (T-5): combat v2's roles, Knacks and elements decide teams; the bonfire only makes sure the whole roster can keep up.
- **V-BON-6** Catch-up toward the server (round 4, Q10): a player whose top team is behind the server's middle (example: the median of the active players' top-team levels) earns faster on every idle gain, fading as they close in (example: up to x2, the same shape as V-BON-2). It runs only on days the player visits, so it never undoes the away steps. A player back after a month is near the middle within about a week of daily visits. Levels still count in the raid (no level sync). With V-BON-2 the total multiplier is capped (example: x3).
- **V-BON-7** The communal hearth (round 4, Q11): the bonfire has a server level, raised like any place (gifts of Acorns, the lamplighter's communal seat, a keystone such as an Ember of the first fire). Each level adds a slight increase to every idle creature's growth on the server ("This could be slightly incremental"; example: +4% a level, +16% at level 5) and something to see: a bigger fire, log benches, lanterns, a ring of stones. Level 1 is the flat floor, so a quiet server is never worse off.
- **V-BON-8** Nothing else to manage: no mood, no food, no Rested mark (F-2, F-10).

## Example numbers (the economy lab sets the real ones)

| What                      | Example                                      |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Bonfire rate, hearth L1   | 4 XP an hour (96 a day)                      |
| Hearth levels             | +4% a level, level 5 at most (+16%)          |
| Roster catch-up (V-BON-2) | x2 at 10+ levels below the roster middle     |
| Server catch-up (V-BON-6) | x2 at 10+ levels below the server middle     |
| Total multiplier cap      | x3                                           |
| Couch with company        | 30 XP an hour while both are in the Activity |

With these, a new catch on day 30 (level 1, the player's middle at 14) earns about 190 a day at the bonfire and reaches level 10 in about 9 days (T-1 wants it "useful within days").

## What it replaces

Today's tight XP limits: the lounge's 60-a-day cap and its Rested mark, treats, and levels mostly from trips and the dungeon (`scrap-list.md`). The economy lab must show the totals against combat v2's level curve before anything is accepted.

## Open (for the panel and the lab)

- Is the bonfire rate high enough that a big roster all grows (G-2), but low enough that a post still feels worth unlocking?
- Is catch-up toward the server fair to the players who played every day, or does it read as a reward for being away?
