# The bonfire: roster-wide growth

Status: **proposed**; the bonfire as a communal hearth and catch-up toward the server are **accepted** (Misa, round 4, Q10 and Q11). Origin: Misa, 2026-10-29: "idle creatures can sit around a bonfire".

## The idea

Creatures without a post are not wasted: they gather at the bonfire in the middle of the valley and grow slowly but steadily. Together with posts, this is the floor that lets a player grow their whole roster (G-2), while fights and posts stay the faster routes.

## Rules (draft)

- **V-BON-1** Every creature a player owns earns XP every hour from one of: a fight (most), a post (steady), the bonfire (the floor). No daily cap on the total (Misa's goal), only the rates and the away steps (V-POST-10: full for a day after a visit, then lower in steps).
- **V-BON-2** Catch-up: a creature below the player's roster middle (example: the median level of their top 10) earns faster at the bonfire and at posts, fading to normal as it nears the middle. New catches become useful within days, not weeks.
- **V-BON-6** Catch-up toward the server (round 4, Q10): a player whose best team is behind the server's middle (example: the median of every active player's top-team level) earns faster on every idle gain while present, fading as they close in. A player back after a month is near the middle within about a week of daily visits. It runs only on days the player visits, so it never undoes the away steps (V-POST-10). Levels still count in the raid (no level sync).
- **V-BON-7** The communal hearth (round 4, Q11): the bonfire has a server level, raised like any place (gifts, a lamplighter post, a keystone). Each level adds a slight increase to every idle creature's growth on the server ("This could be slightly incremental") and something to see (a bigger fire, benches, lanterns). Level 1 is the flat floor, so a quiet server is never worse off.
- **V-BON-3** The bonfire has no seats: every idle creature sits there. On screen it shows a handful around the fire, with "+N" for the rest.
- **V-BON-4** Lounging (today's living room) stays the personal, social version: one creature on the couch, visible to friends, a little more XP and the Rested mark.
- **V-BON-5** Levels are not the main reason to pick a creature (T-5): combat v2's roles, Knacks and elements decide teams; the bonfire only makes sure the whole roster can keep up.

## What it replaces

Today's tight daily XP limits (the lounge's capped XP, levels mostly from fights and training costs). The economy lab must show the new totals against combat v2's level curve before anything is accepted.
