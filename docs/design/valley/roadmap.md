## Order inside phase 1 (current)

1. **Question rounds** with Misa (ongoing; 13 rounds so far) ([`deciding.md`](deciding.md), the ledger [`questions.md`](questions.md)): the foundations first (one valley or many, posts instead of timers, how much opening the game matters), then the **scrap list** ([`scrap-list.md`](scrap-list.md): today's game, keep, rework or scrap), then each system's open choices. The goals in [`goals.md`](goals.md) change with each answer.
2. **Done.** **Posts** and **the bonfire** first: they decide how XP flows, which everything else depends on.
3. **Done.** **The return moment**: what a player sees and decides; it shapes how outputs are reported.
4. **Done.** **Scouts** and **the Forge loop** (and gear, `systems/gear.md`): the first chain (finds feed crafting).
5. **Done.** **The commons** (with collections and the Forge's fire): contributions, server posts, communal place upgrades.
6. **Waiting.** **The School**: last, because it waits on combat v2's talent rework.
7. **Next, if Misa agrees (Q32).** **The economy model** brought up to date with the decisions, then built (phase 2). Pia's measurement lists are in each review in `reviews/`.

## Seams with combat v2 (agree before building)

- Creature XP and levels: the bonfire and posts add XP; combat v2 decides what a level is worth in a fight.
- Talents: the School teaches combat v2's talents; their rules, slots and rarity come from that branch.
- The raid: combat v2 owns the weekend's rules; the valley owns the Watchtower, the muster's place and the defence framing.
- **The roster profile** (from the combat v2 session, 2026-10-30; branch `combat-v2-track`, `packages/lab/src/roster.ts`, `docs/design/combat/boss-track.md`): combat v2 tunes each boss to the roster that arrives at its slot, using, per kind of player (daily, weekly, new with a join week) and per week 1 to 24, the levels of the 1st, 3rd, 10th and 20th creature, roster size, forms available, talent slots filled, and gear (slots usable, item level). Until the economy lab exists it runs on placeholder numbers taken from `systems/posts.md`'s worked examples. The economy lab outputs exactly this shape (`economy/model.md`), or the difference is raised with Misa once.
- **The fixed boss track** (combat v2, 2026-10-30): each Boss Rank has a known boss; a failed boss returns the next week at full HP; the rank rises only on a kill; Festival weeks pause the track. Valley rules that assume a raid pace must say so: the item level cap rising with raid wins (`systems/gear.md` V-GEAR-8, which now means kills), fight XP in the example numbers (combat v2 sets it), and the gear slot unlock levels (combat v2's call).
