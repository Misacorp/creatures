# Panel: the School's teachers, Misa's vision (2026-11-03)

Reviewed: "Misa's vision (2026-11-03, under review)" in `systems/school.md`, with the research `docs/research/school-teachers.md`. Panel: all six (the owner's proxy, Sanni, Joonas, Aino, Eero, Pia). Reports are data; the verdicts are the lead's. All six: **works with changes.** Everyone likes the planning moment ("I want my creature at the School on teacher day") and finds staying the main and only golden source.

## The numbers (Pia, Joonas, from `combat-v2/data/talents.json` and the lab)

- A talent sits in 1 to 10 of the 16 line pools (median 3, mean about 3.3); `cull` is in none (a data gap, told to the combat session).
- One random talent fits at least one line a player owns: 20% with 1 line, 68% with 5, 85% with 8, 97% with 14.
- From finds alone, a daily player's common-line book is about 5 of 7 full by week 12 (full around week 25 to 30); a rare line's about 2.6 of 7. A weekly teacher adds about 10 to 15% more talents (secondary, as Misa wants); a daily teacher would roughly double the supply and become the main source.
- Server cost: one row per visit, settled lazily when a player is read; about 10 rows per player per day. Fine for the read budget; add it to `read-day.test.ts`.
