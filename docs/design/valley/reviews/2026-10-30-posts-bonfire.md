# Panel: job posts and the bonfire (2026-10-30)

Reviewed: `systems/posts.md` and `systems/bonfire.md` as written in full on 2026-10-30 (commit "job posts and the bonfire written in full"). Panel: the owner's proxy, Sanni (casual), Joonas (veteran), Pia (systems). Aino and Eero sat out: no art or screens yet. Each read the goals, Misa's answers and the docs; reports are data, the verdicts are the lead's.

## Verdicts in one line each

- **Owner's proxy:** the bones are right, but the docs pile on number knobs; the evening reads like a to-do list with no real choice.
- **Sanni:** cozy for three days; by week 2 a shop list of upgrades, and half her creatures become "+8" by the fire.
- **Joonas:** calm and fair, but where a creature works is a lookup table, solved in one evening.
- **Pia:** computable lazily, but the example numbers clash: the whole roster hits level 30 by week 10, the couch and server catch-up can be exploited, and Acorns have no income number.

## What the lab must measure first (Pia)

1. Acorns in, spent and stored, per profile and week (seats, kit, lures, commons).
2. The day the 1st, 3rd and 10th creatures reach level 30, per profile (T-6).
3. A weekly against a daily player, idle only and with fights.
4. Days for a returner to get within 2 levels of the server's middle.
5. The spread of output multipliers (fit, skill, kit) between daily, weekly and new players.
6. Rows read per visit and per server day with the frozen-multiplier pass.
