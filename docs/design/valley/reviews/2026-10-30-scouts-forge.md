# Panel: scouts and the Forge (2026-10-30)

Reviewed: `systems/scouts.md` and `systems/forge.md` as written in full on 2026-10-30. Panel: the owner's proxy, Sanni (casual), Joonas (veteran), Pia (systems). Reports are data; the verdicts are the lead's.

## Verdicts in one line each

- **Owner's proxy:** mostly the good kind of chain, but shards hide a second bottleneck the bubble never names, and duplicates become a daily to-do list.
- **Sanni:** the scouts are cozy; the Forge and the Paddock gate become a to-do list by week two.
- **Joonas:** scrap never runs short and the Forge makes more gear than anyone can use, so each step has one obvious answer.
- **Pia:** a usable first draft, but scouts' Acorns grow with every seat bought, the Forge makes about four times the gear a roster can use, and the wrong input runs short.
