# Panel: trials instead of ribbons (2026-10-30)

Reviewed: `systems/trials.md` as first written (Misa's idea from round 15, the lead's sketch and six open choices). Panel: the owner's proxy, Sanni (casual), Joonas (veteran), Pia (systems). Reports are data; the verdicts are the lead's.

## Verdicts in one line each

- **Owner's proxy:** adopt with changes. Scrap the level gates now; trials come back as an honour every creature can earn. "A trial must never stop a creature's levels."
- **Sanni:** adopt with changes. An honour that ticks itself; a ceremony at a set time feels like a timer; 40 creatures times four boxes is homework, so show pips, not lists.
- **Joonas:** adopt with changes. The best part is the creature's résumé ("Pip cleared the Undercroft and made an Epic"); give each trial routes to choose between; drop boxes that only repeat mastery.
- **Pia:** adopt with changes. Gates cost a daily player about 214,000 XP in weeks 1 to 12 (about 27% of idle XP); no fight-only boxes (3 raid seats a week against 47 creatures at week 12); ribbons are a sink of about 1,500 scrap and 500 shards per player by week 12, which must go somewhere else.

Nobody hates it. All four say the same thing first: **an honour, not a gate.**
