# Panel: the commons (2026-10-30)

Reviewed: `systems/commons.md` as written in full on 2026-10-30 (projects, collections, the Forge's fire). Panel: the owner's proxy, Sanni (casual), Joonas (veteran), Pia (systems). Reports are data; the verdicts are the lead's.

## Verdicts in one line each

- **Owner's proxy:** it serves Misa's goals, and the fire and collections each fit in one sentence, but the pacing numbers don't add up, one rule invites guilt, and parts are over-built.
- **Sanni:** cozy and mostly guilt-free, but the weekly goal, the thank-you threshold and collections her keen friends fill quietly sort her into "didn't help".
- **Joonas:** good structure, but the fire may not change anything he crafts, time warms it as much as players do, and a big giver looks the same as someone who gave once.
- **Pia:** five places split one pot of about 150 Acorns a day, so the pace claims are off by two to ten times; the fire's start is not stated; the hoarding rule has a hole.
