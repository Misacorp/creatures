# Panel: the weekly visitor (2026-10-30)

Reviewed: `systems/visitor.md` (Misa's idea from round 16, Q42, and the lead's draft from `docs/research/weekly-visitor.md`). Panel: the owner's proxy, Sanni (casual), Joonas (veteran), Pia (systems). Reports are data; the verdicts are the lead's. All four: **adopt with changes.**

## Verdicts in one line each

- **Owner's proxy:** keep him until the raid finale; sell Chaos Orbs to keep instead of the leave-it-in-advance stall (closer to Misa's "orbs", simpler, no babysitting); all lines, no "keep the old one"; launch stock is orbs and one rare blueprint; no decorations or lures yet (art and new systems).
- **Sanni:** a lovely weekly beat; the drop-off is kind; let me keep the old version or I'll never press the button; one flat price; no countdown or "until Saturday" deadline wording.
- **Joonas:** stay until the Sunday finale so weekend players meet him; all lines with real risk; the rising price makes "when to stop" a decision; one thing each visit you can't get anywhere else; post the reveal to the server's Discord channel; turn the drop-off into a standing order ("reroll up to N times until a line I pick appears").
- **Pia:** a base price rising x1.5 per reroll, reset weekly, drains about one daily player's weekly surplus in three rerolls; scale the price with item level so it keeps up with income; take shards of any element (the piece's own element would worsen the Forge's shard waits); kept orbs must count toward the week's rising price; about 4 rows per reroll.
