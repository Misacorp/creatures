# The economy lab in plain words (2026-10-31)

For the phase 2 gate ("the targets pass for every player profile; the report reads sensibly to Misa", `roadmap.md`). Written by the lead from the lab's report at commit fb0b3c3 (`report.md`, generated; the charts are on the phone page). Every number is from the simulation of the rules as decided through round 17, for a mixed server of 8 unless it says otherwise. Combat is not simulated: fights pay fixed inputs (about 120 XP a day from the dungeon and 70 from the raid for a top creature), agreed with combat v2.

## What a season looks like

- **A daily player** (one evening visit): about 11 kinds of creature (lines) at week 4, 26 at week 12 and 49 at week 24. Their best creature is level 19 at week 4, 25 at week 8 and 29 at week 12, reaching 30 around day 89; their 10th creature is level 9, 19 and 25 at the same weeks.
- **A weekly player** (Sundays): about 8, 18 and 37 kinds; best creature 16, 22 and 26; their creatures grow at about 82% of a daily player's pace (catch-up holds until the next visit), while their scouts bring home about 41% of a daily player's finds (stores hold three days).
- **A newcomer** joining in week 6 reaches level 30 on day 65 after joining (a day-one player took 89 days): they close the gap without overtaking.
- **The valley** (a server of 8): its first place levels up on day 7, 5 level-ups by week 12, 4 collections done by week 12. The Forge's fire (the item level cap) is about 47 at week 12 if the server kills its bosses, 42 at half and 36 if it kills none; there is no time floor.
- **What a server can pool for the raid** (launch creatures): every common and rare line from week 4; the epics by week 24.
- **The visitor** drains Acorns and scrap: from week 9 a daily player rerolls about once a week and buys his blueprint every week; with him, half of 8-player servers have a near-perfect Epic by week 10.

## What passes

Growth through the season (T-6), weekend off costs almost nothing (T-2b), weekly players grow at 82% (T-2c), the valley's pace and the fire (T-3), collections (T-3), the collection pace by rarity (T-8), Essence never starving evolutions (T-9), nothing dead before week 12 (T-7), and a creature owned two weeks is within 5 levels of the 3rd best (T-1, restated).

## What fails, honestly

1. **Shards at the Forge** (T-4): players who don't visit daily wait for a shard of the right element a lot (every other day: 40% of anvil time; weekend off: 30%; weekly: 71%); a daily player 16%. The exchange only helps when someone uses it. _Your call: Q46._
2. **Your T-1 as written**: the 10th creature is 10 levels below the 3rd at week 4, because a player only has about 11 creatures then and the 10th arrived days ago. A creature owned for two weeks is within 5 levels. _Your call: Q47._
3. **Returners** take 16 days to come within 2 levels of the server's middle (target "about two weeks"). _In Q47._
4. **Scrap piles up again from week 14** (T-7b): after the visitor, nothing late in the season takes enough scrap. You said piling is fine for the first weeks while sinks are built; the next sink is the lead's to propose (more visitor stock, decorations later).
