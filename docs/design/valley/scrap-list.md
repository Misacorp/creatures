# The scrap list: today's game, keep, rework or scrap

Status: **proposed** by the lead (2026-10-29), from an inventory of the shipped game and Misa's answers in rounds 1 and 2. Under G-0 (be brave), nothing is kept because it exists. Rows marked **ask** are in the queue of [`questions.md`](questions.md); the rest are the lead's call unless Misa disagrees. Players who lose something they earned get an "I was there" memento at the migration (roadmap phase 6).

Evidence is thin: no live ops numbers are committed (the balance log's "Live" lines are forecasts), so the evidence is the simulator, Misa's playtests and a few tester reports quoted in `docs/design/decisions.md`.

## What interlocks (from the inventory)

- **Trips were the hub**: the only source of creatures, duplicates, blueprints, most shards, Journal pages and explorer ranks. Scouts must take over every one of those inputs, or the systems behind them starve.
- **Ribbons tie levels to parts**, and parts come from trips, the dungeon and dismantling.
- **The raid is the Essence source**, and Boss Rank drives weather strength, Omens, the Festival and finale gear.
- **The dungeon is the main supply source** (one guaranteed per run).
- **The Games are a closed loop**: medals buy from the Prize Stall, show on cards and trigger a DM. Scrapping the Games takes all of that with them.
- **Bait has a thin purpose** once treats go.
- **Thin layers are cheap**: the Home board, DMs, Discord posts and Stats are views over other systems.
