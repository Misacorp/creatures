## 2026-10-24: Valley Games polish: crowding solved at display time, a calm leaderboard, one icon box

ID: `2026-10-24-valley-games-polish-crowding-solved-at` · Status: current

The "After v1" polish list in `valley-games.md`, Misa's items 1 to 4 and most of "Seen while building".

- **Crowded big fields are a client layout layer, not an engine change.** `games/show-layout.ts` spreads positions when `prepareEvent` reads the stored beats, so results, records and old shows stay as they were. Above 16 solo entrants creatures are drawn at 0.75 size (`is-small`, body only; the "you" marker follows); team events above 8 teams stand in ranks with gaps (Pumpkin Push and Log Carry: wider columns and crews further back; Crate Tower and Kite Flight: three ranks; Bucket Brigade: two, its pond is in front); Melon Run above 8 runners starts in staggered rows of three whose sideways offset fades out over the first 160 px, so the finish is the engine's. Boards rank by the engine's own x (`PosKey.rx`), never the drawn one.
- **A calm leaderboard.** `games/board-calm.ts`: two neighbouring rows swap only after the swap has held for 700 ms of show time (ticks are 250 to 1000 ms, so a one-tick swap never shows and a real overtake lands within about one commentary beat). It is driven by the show clock; a seek back, a jump over 1 s or a new event starts again from the true order, and the last tick and the podium show the exact order. Moves slide (FLIP), off with reduced motion. Board rows are one grid, so the score column never shrinks.
- **Commentary wraps instead of cutting.** The commentary box is always two lines high; a line that does not fit on one wraps to the second, so owners stay and the layout does not jump. Only a line that does not fit in two lines ends in "…".
- **One icon box and the app's spacing scale on the Games tab.** Favour icons and the picker's size marks share `--ge-icon` (1.5 rem); the size marks form one column whether or not the Good fit badge shows. Spacing snaps to the app's 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 rem steps. The Good fit tick sprite lost its opaque square.
- **No level in the Games picker.** `ui/CreatureList` takes `level={false}`; only the Games entry picker uses it.
- **Small items.** Team event cards carry a "Team" glossary tag (`games-team`). The rollup counts `games_entry` and `games_buy` as a `games` feature, adds `games.*` counters and the Prize Stall's medal spend as a flow (medal earnings are not tracked per player yet). `runCore` in the worker tests seeds every live dice (`readinessRng` stays live on purpose).

## 2026-10-23: Three more team events, all played at once: Bucket Brigade, Crate Tower, Kite Flight

ID: `2026-10-23-three-more-team-events-all-played` · Status: current · Amends: `2026-10-23-team-events-are-real-team-sports`

Misa's rule for team events holds: a team event's length never grows with the number of teammates or teams. All five team events are now in the daily draw (`chooseLineup` is unchanged: it picks the day's team event from every live team event).

- **Three new kinds, team only, a fixed time.** `fill` (Bucket Brigade, scene `barrel-pond`), `stack` (Crate Tower, `crate-yard`) and `kite` (Kite Flight, `kite-hill`) run exactly `maxTicks` (100 ticks of 400 ms, 40 s) whatever happens, every team and every member at once. `loadGames` refuses them as solo events. The sim's team-length check (4 against 15 teams) covers them: about 42 s either way.
- **A column per team, a still camera.** Up to 16 teams side by side across the 192 px scene (`columnXs` in `packages/engine/src/games/columns.ts`: 11 px apart at 16, at most 40 px, shuffled so nobody is always at the edge). Each team has one object, a prop per team (`barrel:<i>` f = level in percent, `tower:<i>` f = crates, `kite:<i>` the kite's centre, f 1 tumbling), drawn in the team's colour (`teamRamp`, the secondary ramp of a code-drawn sprite in `art/drawn-team.ts`). The "you" marker sits over your team's object, as in the hauls.
- **Bucket Brigade.** Members shuttle on their own trips: dip (one tick fewer per Support teammate), run up (Speed against the field), pour, run back faster. The element multiplier is the pail's size (Tide 1.3x). A pour may slip unless steadied (a base chance and the dodge): only 30% goes in, and a slip may splash half of what spilled into a neighbouring barrel. An Ember member's pour may steam 1.5% off a neighbour. The actor's meter is its pail (the client draws it full or empty). Score: the barrel in percent; level barrels share.
- **Crate Tower.** The climber is the member that climbs best (Speed, `smallBp` for a young creature), the holder the steadiest, the fetcher the rest; the team's tempo is the members' mean climb and its steadiness their mean HP with the element and role multipliers (a Guard 1.25x, Grove 1.15x). A tower is safe up to `safeCrates` (7) times its steadiness, then each crate over adds 35% to the wobble chance; Grove members cut it (`rootBp`). An unheld wobble topples the top crate and may knock a neighbour's off. Score: crates; at the same height, the tower that got there first ranks first (the first numbers, a plain per-crate wobble, made the end a random walk round each tower's limit: Spearman 0.4).
- **Kite Flight.** Each tick a kite climbs by the team's lift (members' Speed with the element and role multipliers, the mean; Spark 1.25x) and sinks by 8% of its height, so it settles where its lift holds it. Gusts dip a kite unless an anchor holds (a base 20%, 30% per Guard or Mire member, at most two); lightning strikes the highest kite of a team with a Spark member; neighbouring kites within 8 px may tangle unless a member dodges (both drop and stop climbing for 3 ticks). Score: the height at the end, 4 px a metre (an average over the last seconds would have been smoother but would not match the live board at the end).
- **Aptitude.** Each kind's aptitude sits next to its rules (`fillAptitude`, `stackAptitude`, `kiteAptitude`) and `gamesAptitude` calls them. Crate Tower's weighs the hold three quarters and the climb a quarter, which is what decides a tower's height.
- **Commentary.** New keys `fill.*`, `stack.*`, `kite.*` (start, the chaos lines, the lead, "ten seconds left", win and draw). The lead and the last ten seconds are important; spills, splashes, steam, topples, knocks, gusts, zaps and tangles are twists (at most three of a line a show, so 16 teams never crowd out the rest).
- **Simulator.** `pnpm sim:games`: every target of the three new events passes (Bucket Brigade 42.8 s, Spearman 0.83; Crate Tower 42.8 s, 0.65; Kite Flight 42.2 s, 0.66). Records store `percent` and `crates` as units.

## 2026-10-23: Valley Games: no level, a "Good fit" badge, event badges, no auto-entry

ID: `2026-10-23-valley-games-no-level-a-good` · Status: current · Amends: `2026-10-22-valley-games-engine-five-kinds-stats`, `2026-10-22-valley-games-on-the-server-days`

Misa's decisions for the v1 scope (`valley-games.md`, "v1 scope", items 2 to 4).

- **No level in the Games.** `gamesEntrantFrom` (`packages/engine/src/games/entrant.ts`) builds an entrant from the creature's current species only: HP, Attack and Speed are the species' `baseStats` (the level-1 stats of its current form: no growth, no fusion bonus, no gear stat line), dodge comes from that Speed alone, crit from `RULES.combat.luck` and the species' own crit bonus (no gear crit). Element, role and size (`gamesIsSmall`) as before. Of the gear sheet only the Games' chance and trigger lines are read (`GamesMods`: Sidestep, Stubborn, Second Wind, Echo, Payback, Last Stand, Momentum, Riposte, Warm Heart); flat dodge, stat lines and talents' stat effects are ignored. `level` left `GamesEntrant` (nothing showed it; stored days that carry it still replay). The worker's `gamesRoster` and the simulator pass the species and the sheet. The aptitude's reference stats are now the median species (HP 168, Attack 36, Speed 37). The field curve stays: it now compares species.
- **Retuned for it.** Without level the small-creature bonus outweighed the base stats a young creature lacks (level 1-9 creatures won 1.4x their fair share). Every `smallBp` above 1x is cut to 40% of its bonus (Sauna Showdown 2.1x to 1.44x, Shark Derby 1.55x to 1.22x, and so on), and Pepper Panic (`statBp` 8000, `jitterBp` 2500) and Pumpkin Push (`statBp` 7000, `jitterBp` 2000) read their stat more and their luck less, so a good fit still wins clearly more than a random pick.
- **"Good fit", one binary badge, replaces the 1 to 5 pips.** A creature is a good fit for an event when `gamesAptitude` reaches `gamesGoodFitMin(def)`: the aptitude of the species at the top `RULES.games.goodFit.topSpeciesBp` (25%) of all shipped species for that event (`teamTopSpeciesBp`, 35%, in a team event, which takes three), each species once and gearless, ties at the bar included. A Games gear line can lift a creature over it. **Why this method:** the bar comes from the content alone (no stored per-event number), so a new event or species needs no calibration step, and with no level two creatures of a species are equal, so the species is the right unit. **Calibration:** the shares were chosen with `pnpm sim:games` on the economy sim's rosters (8 and 15 players, weeks 1, 4, 12 and 30), counting each player's good fits for every event of every played day: per event the median is 2 to 4 (now 2 to 4 everywhere; Berry Bonanza and Pumpkin Push lowest at 2). 20% left Berry Bonanza at 1; 30% put four events at 5. The server sends only who has it, `next.goodFit: { [eventId]: creatureId[] }`; the picker lists the good fits first, then the rest, each by name, with no score anywhere. The glossary entry is `games-good-fit` (`games-fit` is gone).
- **Event badges.** A creature collects a badge per event id it has won (gold), with a win count: a new table `games_badges (creature_id, event_id, user_id, wins)` (**migration 40**, `games-badges-migration.ts`), written by the award for every member of a first place (a shared first place counts for each; bots never). It is back-filled from the awarded gold placings of real players. A table rather than a read of `games_placings` on every view: the tab is read often, and `games_placings` only grows (the rows_read lesson of the same day). The tab sends the next lineup's badges (`next.badges: { [eventId]: { [creatureId]: wins } }`), the creature route a list (`badges: { eventId, wins }[]`; a retired event's badge stays). Drawn as one generic medallion (`games/art/badge.ts`) tinted per event scene with a 5x5 glyph of the event, from the scene, else the kind, else gold with a star, so a new event gets one without new art. Kept as `{ eventId, wins }` so a larger badge system can grow from it. The creature page shows a shelf of the events won only (no faint outlines for the rest: fewer things to read); the picker shows the creature's badge for that event beside its name.
- **No auto-entry.** `PUT /api/games/auto` and its schema, the lock's auto-fill (`autoFill`, `byAptitude`, `lastRunner`), the `joined`/`auto` fields of the view, the client switch, its glossary entry and its fixture are gone. A player who does not enter that day is not in the show. The `joined` notion went too: a day locks when somebody entered it, and the alarm arms the next lock while a day not locked yet has entries. The `auto` columns of migration 39 stay, unused (released migrations are frozen). Staging bots are entered by the dev tools (`games-bots`, and `games-run-now` before it locks), never by the lock.
- **Simulator** (`packages/sim/src/games-study.ts`). Policies `fit` (at random among the free good fits, then among the rest), `best-level` (highest level first) and `random`. New targets: level does not predict a win (level 1-9 win share 0.8 to 1.2x their fair share of entries, where an entry's fair share is one over its field size, so the small week-1 fields do not count as level; level 30+ at most 1.2x), highest level first wins at most 1.2x as often as random, good-fit picks win at least 1.5x as often as random in every event, and a median of 2 to 4 good fits per roster in every event. "Strongest" is now by the species' base power, and its 5% floor is not applied to events that favour small creatures (the strongest by base stats is a grown one there, and the young are meant to beat it). Pepper Panic and Pumpkin Push join Floe Sumo with their own Spearman floor (0.5): luck-heavy, and every creature of one species ties on aptitude, which caps a rank correlation. Results (40 days): every target passes (129 of 129); level 1-9 1.18x, level 30+ 0.69x, best-level 1.11x random; good-fit picks win about 2x to 7x as often as random.

## 2026-10-23: Team events are real team sports: Log Carry and Pumpkin Push; Sauna Stack and Shark Relay retired

ID: `2026-10-23-team-events-are-real-team-sports` · Status: current, amended by `2026-10-23-three-more-team-events-all-played` · Amends: `2026-10-22-valley-games-engine-five-kinds-stats`, `2026-10-23-valley-games-polish-commentary-by-priority`

Misa: "Could we drop the sauna and ice room to just one per player? I don't think all the events work as team sports, but something like pushing a boulder or carrying a big item easily works." Then: a team event's length must not grow with the number of teammates or teams. One team event a day stays (three creatures means more bench experience), but only real team sports.

- **Retired: Sauna Stack and Shark Relay.** Sauna Stack sat each team as a stack in one seat; Shark Relay swam the legs one after another, so it grew longer per teammate. Both are out of the pool, and their stacked and relay code (stack seats, levels and heights; legs, tags and the shore) is gone from the engine (`heightBp` and `legLaps` left the schema). Endure and swim are solo kinds now. Stored days, results, records and replays may still name them: `GAMES_RETIRED` in `@creatures/game` keeps each one's name, kind, scene, team size, tick and line, `gamesEventInfo` and `gamesEventName` read it, the worker's shown lineup and creature records use it, and the client names, scores and replays them (the kinds' commentary templates stay, `swim.tag` included). The next day's lineup never offers one. Ids are never reused.
- **New kind `haul`, team only.** Two skins: **Log Carry** (`mode: carry`, scene `log-trail`): three creatures in a row under a birch log; the team's speed is its members' Speed against the field, the slowest counting most (`minWeightBp` 60%, the rest the mean), so a team must match; a team short of members is slower (`shortBp`). **Pumpkin Push** (`mode: push`, scene `pumpkin-hill`): three creatures one behind the other behind a giant pumpkin; Attack adds up with diminishing returns (the strongest whole, then 60% and 35%); big creatures and Mire push best. Both: the element, role and size multipliers as every kind; a Setter in the team leads (`leadBp`); now and then a load wobbles and one member keeps it steady (a base chance, its dodge, each Guard of the team); a Sidestep line steps clear, a Stubborn one catches the load once; else it drops (the log falls, the pumpkin rolls back) and the team stands a few ticks, one fewer per Support and for a Second Wind; a drop may bump a team in a nearby lane; a Spark member's zap jolts its team forward. All numbers are params, integer math.
- **Simultaneous and capped.** Every lane races at once; the race ends `graceTicks` (4 s) after the first team crosses, or at `maxTicks` (44 s). Ranking: finish order, then distance; every member shares its team's score. The new sim check (`teamLengths`) plays each team event with 4 and with 15 teams on the 15-player rosters: the means must be within 10% (now about 41 s against 39 s).
- **Readability.** One lane per team (feet y 48 to 100); the load is a prop per team (`log:<i>`, `pumpkin:<i>`, f: up, wobbling, down) and the members stand round it at fixed offsets. A field of the same speed bunched into one pile, so the lanes start staggered like a running track: up to four start columns (two teams each while that is enough), each 40 px further on, and each lane's finish moved as far (`finish:<i>` props, drawn as a short chequered strip per lane), so every lane is as long. The camera frames the whole field with the leader in view. One "you" marker per team, over its log or its pushers.
- **Commentary.** New keys `haul.start`, `drop`, `catch`, `steady`, `dodge`, `cheer`, `zap`, `bump`, `lead`, `finish`, `close` (a photo finish), `win`, `draw`. Lead changes and finishes are important; drops, catches, bumps, zaps and steadies are twists (capped at three a show, so a big field's many drops never crowd out the start and the finish).
- **Lineup.** The daily team event is drawn from Log Carry and Pumpkin Push; `chooseLineup` is unchanged.
- **Simulator.** `pnpm sim:games`: every target passes, the two new events included (Log Carry 38 s, strongest wins 35%, Spearman 0.85; Pumpkin Push 41 s, strongest wins 34%, Spearman 0.65).

## 2026-10-23: Valley Games polish: commentary by priority, paced exits, real finishes, stacks and a relay

ID: `2026-10-23-valley-games-polish-commentary-by-priority` · Status: current, amended by `2026-10-23-team-events-are-real-team-sports`

Round 3 of the Games engine (the 2026-10-22 entry above).

- **Commentary is chosen when the show is finished, by priority:** exits and the race for the win first, then twists (Steam, a ghost round, a zap, a save), then filler (a gulp, a burp, "does not budge"). An important line waits up to 1.5 gaps for room, a twist half a gap, a filler not at all; a filler is used at most twice a show. Two exits close together share one line ("{a} and {b} ..."). A line never names a creature after it has left (its own exit line aside), and nothing but a last must-say line and the closing line comes after the crowning.
- **Exits are paced** (`ExitGate`): at most a quarter of the field within any 2 s; in a field of 12 or fewer, or once four or fewer are left, at least 1.5 s between two exits. A creature held back stays in at the very edge for a moment. So the commentary can name almost every exit and a crowd never leaves at once.
- **Size is a young creature's:** small means stage 1 of a line that still evolves. A single-stage line (Lanternfin, Hearthshell) is full grown and counts as big; before this, those creatures were "small" at any level and took most of the small bonus.
- **The stat curve has a floor:** a creature far below the field counts as half of it (the clamp was a quarter), so the weakest are never hopeless.
- **Melon Run has a finish line:** runners who cross are safe, ranked by finish order; the melon then smashes into the arch and the race is over. **Sauna Stack** teams sit stacked in one seat (Guards at the bottom shelter those above, higher is hotter, the ones above drop down when one leaves). **Shark Relay** is a real relay: one swimmer per team in the water at a time, tagging the next one in; a bitten-out swimmer ends its team's relay. Floe Sumo's floe grows with a field of more than 16 (up to the art's 84 px), stops shrinking at its smallest and the shoves get stronger instead. One or two entrants make a show of about 25 s.
- **New sim targets:** at least 70% of exits named (fields of 12 or fewer), at most 10% of shows with more than 8 s without a moment, at most 5% of shows with a quarter of the field out within 2 s, Floe Sumo's Spearman at least 0.45 (the chaos event). The strongest-by-power target misses in the HP events: their fit is a high-HP tank (a level 27 Thornbuck Guard) over the strongest glass cannon (a level 30 Tempestray Payoff), which is the feature's intent; the lead decides whether to keep that target for HP events.

## 2026-10-22: Valley Games on the server: days, auto-entry, awards and the Prize Stall

ID: `2026-10-22-valley-games-on-the-server-days` · Status: current, amended by `2026-10-23-games-migration-renumbered-to-39`, `2026-10-23-no-valley-games-results-post`, `2026-10-23-valley-games-no-level-a-good`

The worker side of the Games (`apps/worker/src/games*.ts`, migration 38). Choices made while building it:

- **A show day is a guild-local date D.** Entries for D are open from D-1's show to D's lock (13:37, the daily reset); the show is at 13:40 (`games_days.show_at`, stored at the lock so the dev tools can move it). The "next" show day is the first day whose show has not started, so entries for D+1 open the moment D's show starts. D's lineup is drawn with the live Games RNG the first time anything needs it (`chooseLineup`, avoiding D-1's events).
- **The lock is one transaction** at D's reset: auto-entry, snapshots (the raid's fold through `gamesEntrantFrom`), one seed from the live RNG (`drawSeed`), each event run with `createSeededRng(<seed>:<event id>)`. The frozen entrants and each result are stored per event (`games_results`, one row per event so no row nears the storage's row limit), with the placings. A day locks only if somebody had joined before its lock, so a brand-new server shows nothing until the first lock after someone entered. A late lock (a missed alarm, a reset day) starts its show 15 s after the lock, or at 13:40 if later, rather than in the past.
- **Auto-entry.** A player joins with their first entry; auto-entry is on by default (`PUT /api/games/auto`). At the lock, every event a joined auto player left empty gets the creature(s) that ran that event id last time if still theirs and free, then their best free creatures by `gamesAptitude`, in lineup order (solo events first, the team event last, filled to three). A team the player set themselves is left as they set it, even if short. Bots (the staging dev guests) are always auto.
- **Entries** are a set (`PUT /api/games/entry`, no idempotency key). A creature already in another event that day is refused (`already_entered`) rather than moved, so the client always says what happens. Over the cap (24, or 48 creatures in a team event) players come before bots, each in entry order, and a team is kept or left out whole.
- **Awards at the show's end** (an alarm candidate; `awarded_at` makes them run once): medals by the real place (a bot ahead of a player does not move them up; bots themselves get no medal, record or experience), a pin per creature that beat its own record in that event id (its first entry only sets the record), `gamesXp` per creature, the pocket in medal points, then the podium post (`games:<day>:podium`). The post is queued at the award, never before, so it cannot spoil the show.
- **Results are never sent before `show_at`.** `GET /api/games` gives the last show that has started (with `serverNow`, the timeline and `endsAt`) until the next one starts; the player's own results (`you.lastResults`) only once a show is awarded. The show's length is `showTimeline` in `packages/game` (an intro card of 4 s per event, its play, a podium card of 5 s): the client must use the same function so the awards land as the podium ends.
- **The Prize Stall** (`games-stall.json`): five offers (a rare mystery gear piece at the player's dungeon item level 9 points, a good supply 4, 30 Bait 3, 3 shards 3, 1 glimmer 4), each with a weekly limit, all offered every Wednesday game day (no rotation yet: the content can change). Purchases go through `runKeyed` and the existing `payOnce` grant (source `games`, item origin `games`), with what they roll drawn from the live gear RNG at the purchase.
- **Analytics:** `games_entry`, `games_show` and `games_buy` events, and `games` as a creature-experience source.

## 2026-10-22: Valley Games engine: five kinds, stats against the field, a timeline the client only plays

The simulation core of the Valley Games (`docs/design/valley-games.md`). Code: `packages/engine/src/games/`, content `games-events.json`, rules `RULES.games` and `rules/games.ts`, study `pnpm sim:games`.

- **One pure engine, a presentation-ready timeline.** `runGamesEvent(def, entrants, rng)` resolves an event at the lock with the server's crypto RNG and returns beats (positions, effects, meters, scores, states, eliminations, props, commentary keys), the actors' start positions and the standings. The client positions sprites and plays effects; it decides nothing. Integer math only (basis points, a fixed integer sine table); deterministic for a seeded RNG, so a stored show replays exactly.
- **Five kinds, events are content.** `endure` (sauna), `chase` (melon), `feast` (peppers), `swim` (sharks), `shove` (floe). Each event is a kind with its own numbers (`params`), an element table, optional role and size multipliers, a scene and commentary overrides; a skin like the Frost Bath needs no code.
- **Stats count against the field, with diminishing returns.** Each kind reads its key stat as `sqrt(stat / median of the field)` (clamped to a quarter to four times), weighted per event by `statBp`, times the element, role and size multipliers. So a creature twice the median is not twice as good, and every event has a clear "this is the creature for it" (an element, a role, small size) that is not just level.
- **The end crowns the winner.** Every show ends with the standings' first place (every member of a winning team) cheering and the closing line naming it; a shared first place gets the draw line. In a feast the last one standing eats on until it bursts too, so outlasting everyone wins.
- **Team events cap at 48 creatures** (`RULES.games.maxTeamEntrants`, 16 teams of three) while solo events cap at 24: a 15-player server must fit a team event. A team's score is its best member's (`teamScore: best`) or the sum (`sum`).
- **The result carries each actor's own score and elimination tick** (`scores`, `outAt`), so the worker can keep personal records per creature (pins) and the simulator can measure when eliminations happen.
- **Nine events**, the spec's seven plus Berry Bonanza (a sour feast where Grove is hardy) and Shark Relay (a second team event). With a single team event of the endure kind and "no kind twice a day", the solo endure events could never be drawn.
- **Tuning targets** (`pnpm sim:games`, economy-sim rosters of 8 and 15 players at weeks 1, 4, 12 and 30, players' entry policies mixed): per event, the strongest entrant by power wins 15-45%, a bottom-half entrant reaches the podium in at least 15% of events, 20-50 s, at least one moment every 4 s, aptitude still predicts results (Spearman at least 0.6) and aptitude picks win at least 1.5x as often as random ones. Across solo events, creatures 4th or lower in their own roster take at least 15% of wins, level 1-9 creatures win at least 0.4x their share of entries and level 30+ at most 1.5x. First numbers pass all but Floe Sumo's Spearman (about 0.5: the floe is the luckiest event).
