## 2026-10-28: One spacing scale inside drawers and sheets

ID: `2026-10-28-one-spacing-scale-inside-drawers-and` · Status: current

The popover pass (above) done for drawers and sheets. A measured pass over 155 drawers and sheets the workbench can open (1313 gaps between stacked blocks) found the same cause: gaps came in seven steps (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16px) with no rule; about 110 vertical margins were set by the parts (item card, send form, evolve, dungeon rooms, pickers, place card), global `p`/`h*`/`label` margins leaked in, and grids with a `gap` plus margins on their children doubled it (the wizard added 8px under its Back row, the evolve footer padded itself inside the drawer's own footer padding, a choose-list row was 8px from the next while a pick-list row was 4px). A button sat 4px under the creature list (fold row) and 6px under the reason line of the send footer; the evolve stats floated up to 51px under the ability text; the sheet had its own padding and status margins.

- **The scale** (tokens on the root, `drawer.css`): `--dr-tight` 4px (in a group, between rows), `--dr-gap` 8px (between blocks), `--dr-action` 12px (above any button on its own line, the pinned footer's too: one rule for space above a button everywhere), `--dr-section` 16px (between sections and steps), `--dr-pad` 12px (sides, bottom, a sheet all round). 6px and 10px are gone. The popover's 4/8/12 are the same steps. The tokens are on `:root` (not on `.drawer-panel`) because the creature rows use them in pages too.
- **Structure:** the body, toolbar, footer and sheet are stacks, and `dr-stack` / `dr-group` / `dr-section` mark parts inside them (`:nth-child` weight, mirrored from `popover.css`). The body alone pads the top (8px under the heading); head and toolbar padding below went to 0 (the toolbar pads its own top). Parts that drew their own spacing lost it: item card and item drawer, wizard head and steps, send form (each step is a `dr-section dr-group`), job drawer, reach options, recipe card, haul strip, evolve (the ability grows to line the numbers up instead of an auto margin), dungeon rooms, place card, trip rows, the item picker's detail card, dismantle. Shared parts that a page also draws (`.actions`, `.reason`, `.drill-head`, `.line-list`) keep their margin for the page; inside a stack the stack's rule outweighs it. An empty live region adds no step.
- **Visible changes beyond spacing:** the item card's name and tags are one block and the stats sit 8px under it; the item drawer's Prev/Next row sits 8px (was 16) above the card; evolve cards' inner gaps are 8px (were 6) and their numbers still line up; choose-list rows are 4px apart (were 8); the sheet is padded 12px all round (was 8/12/12) and its title-to-list gap is 8px.
- **Prevention:** `ui/drawer-spacing.test.ts` (CI) fails when a drawer-only stylesheet (or a drawer-only class in a shared one) sets a vertical margin, and checks the tokens, the stack rules and the single body padding. `scripts/overlay-audit.mjs` (the popover audit generalised, `--kind popover|drawer|sheet|all`; `popover-audit.mjs` stays as a wrapper) opens every popover, drawer and sheet of the workbench in a browser and checks buttons, stack steps, edges, left and right alignment and own margins; it ends `AUDIT CLEAN` for all kinds. Not in CI (no browser); in the "Showing work to Misa" checklist. A new drawer or sheet entry goes in `OVERLAY_ENTRIES` in the script.

## 2026-10-28: One spacing scale inside popovers

ID: `2026-10-28-one-spacing-scale-inside-popovers` · Status: current

Misa saw the "More in How to play" button touching the text of a glossary popover. A measured pass over all 168 popovers the workbench can open found the cause and more: every popover part set its own margins, and a broad `.popover p` rule outweighed most of them, so gaps came out as 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10px with no rule (the link sat 2px under the text in the 25 glossary entries with no lines; the creature popover had a doubled gap at the bottom; a bold answer sat 2px over its list).

- **The scale** (tokens on `.popover`): 4px inside a group, 8px between groups, 12px above a button on its own line, the same padding on every side. Written up in `ux-patterns.md`, "Spacing inside a popover".
- **Stacks, not margins:** the popover, `pop-body` and `pop-stack` space their children (`:nth-child` gives the rules the weight of two classes, so a shared part's own margin never wins); `PopoverCard` puts the title and the short answer in one `pop-heading` group. Parts set no outer margins. Custom content (the item card's popover, the medal table) uses `pop-stack`; the forecast uses the tokens.
- **Prevention:** `ui/popover-spacing.test.ts` (CI) fails when any stylesheet gives a popover part (`.pop-*`) a vertical margin of its own; `scripts/popover-audit.mjs` taps every popover trigger in the workbench and measures the gaps in a real browser (it found the original bug when run on the old CSS). The audit is in the "Showing work to Misa" checklist next to the overflow audit; it is not in CI because CI has no browser.

## 2026-10-27: Bulk selection replaces marking

ID: `2026-10-27-bulk-selection-replaces-marking` · Status: current · Amends: `2026-10-17-item-corners-everywhere-and-tags-that`, `2026-10-27-marked-items-dismantled-as-a-pile`

Misa tested the marked-items build (above) on staging: he liked the interaction and the results screen, but testers read the "Dismantle items" button as "dismantle now". He asked for a general multi-select with bulk actions, started by gesture, plus a direct Dismantle on one item. This replaces the UI and the persisted marks of the entry above; the pile rules (rolls, XP, Market Day, ledger, no Workshop slot) stand.

1. **No marks.** Migration 49 drops `gear_items.dismantle_mark` (48 already ran on staging and is untouched; `dismantle_days` stays). `POST /api/gear/mark`, `markedForDismantle`, the "marks changed" 409 and the "equip clears the mark" logic are gone. `POST /api/gear/dismantle { creatureId, itemIds, idempotencyKey }` names the items; same key, same stored result.
2. **Worn items go too**, taken off by the same transaction (the row is deleted). The dismantler's own gear (Tinker's luck, Apprentice) is read before any item comes off, which is also what the preview showed. An item in an open job of a removed kind is still 409 `item_in_job`.
3. **Away creatures.** Equipping is frozen for a creature that is away or on a job (its claim reads gear live). A dismantle may still take an item off such a creature only if that changes nothing already started. Checked per activity (`dismantle-worn.ts`): a **trip** fixes its time (Quick feet) and ticks (Crossroads Wanderer) when sent but its claim reads Trail XP, Keen nose, Lucky find, Packer, Second look and the glimmer find live; a **Workshop job** fixes time (Busy paws), level, odds and mods at start but its claim reads Apprentice and, for an old dismantle job, Tinker's luck live; a **dungeon run** freezes each creature's folded gear into its stored input and `gear` ids (a creature that joins late is read live, excluding items the run carries), a **raid** phase or the live finale stores its fight inputs when built, and the **Games** snapshot entrants at the lock, so none of those read worn items again. The check folds the creature's rules with and without the item and compares only the fields its running activity reads live, so sets and caps are exact; items fine one by one but changing a capped rule together are all held. A held item is 409 `item_locked` (nothing taken, no detail). `POST /api/gear/dismantle-check { itemIds }` answers `{ locked: [{ itemId, creatureId, reason: trip | job }] }` read-only, so the warning step can say it before the creature is picked.
4. **Client.** No mode button, presets or "Done". Gesture, checkbox, Ctrl/Shift, right-click menu, keyboard and the live region as in `ux-patterns.md` "Bulk actions". Flow: worn warning (one creature row per wearer, "Dismantle anyway", Back; held items say why in their row and the button is off) when anything is worn, then a free creature, then the confirmation, then the results screen. The item card has a red Dismantle (last) running the same flow for one item. The one-time hint ("Press and hold an item to select several" on touch, "Click the box on an item to select several" with a mouse) is retired by dismissing it or selecting anything, per device in `localStorage` with in-session fallback (the same rule as `coach.ts`).
5. **Not testable here, needs the real Discord client:** the long press on iOS and Android (contextmenu and callout suppression, the buzz), the right click inside Discord desktop's iframe, and sticky bar placement above the real dock.

**UI revision after Misa's phone test of PR #94 (2026-10-28).** He liked the flow and asked for three changes, which replace the UI details of point 4 above:

1. **The worn step uses the established creature row** (`ui/CreatureList`, new `show` mode: a plain list nothing is picked from): one row per wearing creature (picture, name, level, element and role) with its item or items as small tiles on the right (`PickerOption.worn` may now be a list); a held item keeps its reason as the row's quiet line.
2. **Selection is a square checkbox.** The round circle became a square, square-cornered box (24 px drawn, in a 32 px button at the tile's top left: Misa found 44 px took too much of the tile on a phone, 2026-10-28) with the existing pixel `check` glyph (the one `PlacedCount` draws) on a chosen tile, which also keeps its gold ring. The small "i" is gone: a tap on the tile's body always opens the card (also while selecting, over the selection); the checkbox toggles. It is a button of its own with `role="checkbox"`, `aria-checked` and the label "Select Kelp Cap, item level 5", out of the tab order (Space on the tile still toggles, Enter opens). The slot icon of a bag tile moved to the bottom left so the box never covers it.
3. **One flow, one frame.** The card's Dismantle had its own `DismantleInline` (the same wizard inside the card's scrolling content: the gold button not pinned, a different header). It is deleted: the Workshop swaps the card's drawer for `DismantleDrawer` (the same component the bulk bar opens; Back from the first step returns to the card; the swap does not slide in again). Rule in `ux-patterns.md`; guards: `dismantle-frame.test.tsx` (both entry points draw the same drawer, head and pinned footer) and `wizard-footer-guard.test.tsx` (every `WizardFooter` in every workbench preset sits in a `Drawer` or `SidePanel` footer slot).

**Drawer: a pull on the handle is the page's gesture (2026-10-28).** On Misa's Android phone, closing a drawer by pulling its handle down left the page deaf to taps for about half a second; tapping outside, desktop and Chromium's phone emulation had no delay. Best explanation: Discord's Android app around the page also took the pull down as its own gesture and let it settle. The handle now marks its touch moves handled (`preventDefault` from a non-passive `touchmove` listener, only while a pull is under way and only when the browser allows it), which tells a WebView's host to keep out; a tap still closes. One fix in `ui/Drawer.tsx` covers every drawer. Misa confirmed on the phone that the delay is gone. A custom touch drag in a drawer or elsewhere should do the same.

## 2026-10-26: While you were away: missed rewards on the notice board

ID: `2026-10-26-while-you-were-away-missed-rewards` · Status: current

Misa: "accumulate missed rewards from older activities into a single place and show that to the user via the notice board after they log back on after a long period of inactivity ... efficiently, and compile loot from many sources."

- **Recorded at payout, never found by scanning history.** Migration 46 adds `away_rewards`: one row per player and source kind (`leet`, `raid`, `games`) with summed integer counts (Bait, Essence, finds, creatures, medals, XP) and the first and last payout time. Each automatic payout calls `recordAway` (`while-away.ts`) in the transaction that pays it: one UPSERT, gated in the same statement by `INSERT ... SELECT ... FROM players WHERE user_id = ? AND last_seen_at < ?`, so a player who is around records nothing and the gate is one primary-key lookup.
- **Away means unseen for more than 24 hours** at the moment of the payout (`AWAY_AFTER_MS`, a worker constant). The payouts run in the alarm, not in a visit, and a payout reads `last_seen_at` as it is then, so opening the game later (which moves `last_seen_at`) cannot hide what was paid before it.
- **Hooked payouts (everything the server pays without the player acting):** the daily leet Bait (`leet-sync.ts`); the raid's stage Essence, the stage experience, the finale's gear piece or blueprint, the jackpot Essence, and the Festival gear and creature (`raid-resolve.ts`); the Valley Games' medals and experience (`games-award.ts`). Not hooked, on purpose: the daily allowance (paid by the player's own first request, with its own notice), lounge XP (only paid while present), trips, Workshop jobs and rank gifts (the player claims them), and the one-time level reset refund (its own card). To add a source, see the header of `while-away.ts`.
- **Waiting, not recorded.** Finished trips and Workshop jobs are claimed by the player, so nothing was paid. The summary counts them at read time (`tripsWaiting`, `jobsWaiting`) with the user's trip and job indexes, and only when the player has something recorded.
- **Read once, cheaply.** `GET /api/state` (and every action answer that embeds the state) carries `awayRewards`, read by primary-key prefix: no rows, no cost. The counts for trips and jobs are two more indexed reads, only for a player with rows. `POST /api/away/dismiss` takes `{ until }` (the summary's last payout), is strict, uses the session's user and is idempotent. It deletes rows whose last payout is not after `until`, so a payout that landed after the card was drawn is shown next time. The daily maintenance drops rows older than 90 days.
- **The card** is a paper on the Today board above "Needs you" (`home/away-notice.ts`, `NoticeBoard`): a row per source ("Daily gifts", "Raid", "Valley Games") with count badges, one line for what waits ("2 trips and 1 job are waiting for you") and a gold "Got it". The card appears only when a source paid something; trips and jobs alone already have their "Needs you" rows. The game never says "leet", so the Bait source is called "Daily gifts".

## 2026-10-22: Pixel scrollbars on desktop, the system's own on phones

ID: `2026-10-22-pixel-scrollbars-on-desktop-the-system` · Status: current

Misa asked for scrollbars in the arcade look. `styles/base/scrollbar.css` styles every scrollbar, but only for a mouse (`hover: hover` and `pointer: fine`). The bar is 5 UI pixels wide: a night track in an ink frame, and a haze thumb with the panels' bevel that turns gold under the mouse.

- **Built with `::-webkit-scrollbar`.** Discord's desktop client is Chromium, and the standard `scrollbar-color` only sets two flat colours, which cannot make a pixel bevel. Mobile games hide the scrollbar or rely on that indicator; a permanent bar on touch looks cramped and out of place. A themed bar is not worth it there. Where content is cut off with no other cue, a peek or an edge fade (`ui/ScrollHint`) tells the player there is more.
- **Boxes that hide their bar on purpose keep it hidden:** the world map, the living room and the scroll hint.
- **One scrollbar at a time.** While anything with `aria-modal` is open (a drawer, the roster sheet, How to play), the page behind it does not scroll and drops its scrollbar (`html:has([aria-modal='true']) { overflow: hidden }`). Only the drawer's own bar shows. The page keeps its scroll position and gets its bar back on close. Misa saw two bars side by side in a narrow desktop window.

## 2026-10-18: The scroll hint (a reusable building block)

ID: `2026-10-18-the-scroll-hint-a-reusable-building` · Status: current

Misa: players may not notice that the picker's detail panel scrolls, because it has no scrollbar on purpose. One reusable piece in `apps/activity/src/ui/`: `use-scroll-hint.ts` (the hook `useScrollHint(ref, contentKey, options)`), `ScrollHint.tsx` (a box that uses it and draws the fade), `scroll-hint-logic.ts` (the rules as pure functions) and `styles/components/scroll-hint.css`.

- **Behaviour.** When the content overflows, the box rests `delayMs` (2500), then the hook moves it down at `speed` px a second (12), one `requestAnimationFrame` at a time with a fractional position of its own (the browser rounds `scrollTop`), until the bottom, and stops. It never scrolls up. It is over for that content at the first `pointerdown`, `touchstart`, `wheel`, `keydown`, focus inside, or a scroll it did not cause (the box is not where the hint last put it, with 1.5 px of room for rounding). A new `contentKey` (an item selected or flipped to, or the compare switched) starts it again from the top, every time, with no memory. A box whose content fits sets no timer and asks for no frame; timers, frames and listeners go on every content change and on unmount. A long gap between frames (a hidden tab) counts as at most 100 ms, so it never jumps.
- **Look.** The scrollbar stays hidden. While there is more below, a soft fade (1.25 rem, in `--scroll-hint-bg`, the panel's colour) shows at the bottom edge; it is on generally, not only under reduced motion. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (or `reducedMotion: true`) nothing is scrolled and the fade is the hint.
- **Use.** The item picker's detail panel (`ip-detail-scroll`, keyed by the item and the compare switch), and the workbench page `ScrollHint`. The picker's drawer footer no longer scrolls on its own (`max-height: none`), so the action row can never scroll away.

## 2026-10-18: flipping between creatures and items
