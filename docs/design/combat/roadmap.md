# Combat v2 roadmap: from the lab to the game

Status: agreed with Misa, 2026-10-26. How the new combat system replaces the old one: eight phases, each ending at a gate that says when it is good enough to go on. Phases 1 to 3 may overlap. The proposal (`proposals/001-combat-v2.md`) holds the rules; this page holds the order.

## The migration (Misa, 2026-10-26)

Players keep their creatures: species, form, level, ribbons, evolutions, fusions, nicknames, quirks, shinies and private notes. Everything that changes is rolled fresh with live randomness, as if the creature had just been found:

- **Talents** roll from the new line pools (the Talent Book starts again). A golden talent rolls golden (Misa, 2026-10-27). Each line's Talent Book then holds every talent the player's creatures of that line had (golden kept); each creature has learned the ones it had.
- **Knacks:** every creature knows its form's Knacks (R11) and has none equipped (P-14): players choose.
- **Finale abilities** go.
- **Raid planning resets**: placements, the open week, the after-action history stays readable under its old rules.
- **The dungeon run in progress** finishes under the old rules, or is voided (decide in phase 6).
- **A one-time notice** explains the change, like the level reset's card.

Open for Misa before phase 6:

1. **Boss Rank:** keep each server's rank, or reset everyone to rank 1?
2. **Gear:** convert existing items to the new lines (the proposal's gear plan), or reroll them completely like talents?

## How we know it is good

Three layers, from cheapest to most real:

1. **The lab** (automatic, every change): the report's gates in phase 1, the tests.
2. **The panel** (every slice): four reviewers against the principles; findings with verdicts in `reviews/`.
3. **Play** (Misa, testers, then players): the lab page now, staging in phases 4 and 7, live signals after phase 8.

Big simulations come in phase 5 on purpose: week-by-week and Boss Rank numbers mean something only once every species, boss and foe exists in the new system.

**What stands, in short:**

- **Raids** (Misa): a boss the server failed returns the next week at full HP, with last week's best on its HP bar; a kill moves the server along a fixed **boss track** (Tidal Colossus, Cinder Wyrm, Storm Sovereign, Old Bogback, Thornmother as a deliberate wall at the end of the first lap, then again with stronger cards). Festival weeks pause the track; the rank no longer eases after one. Week 1 is a tutorial boss with no rule cards, week 2 has one card, the full set from week 3.
- **Measure:** raids are tuned on **damage potential** (the weekend played as if the boss could not die): a prepared team reaches 125% or more of the boss's HP, an unprepared but decent one 70% to 90% (30% to 60% at the wall); a boss's tool is worth potential points that rise with its card strength (it asks before it requires). Mixed servers are information. Raid weekends are a cliff (the kill flips over 5% to 28% of the HP), which Misa wants: the puzzle decides which side a team is on.
- **Luck is a build:** a crit doubles; crit machines (Cindle, Pyrelynx, Coralynx), crit talents and Knacks, Crit Charges from dodges and from damage a Guard takes; boss rule cards Lucky Strike and Slippery exist but are on no boss yet.
- **Determination** (+5% a failed week, 10 stacks) is built into the path simulator, off; decide when the valley lands.
- **The valley seam:** the roster profile and the server coverage by rarity come from the valley's economy lab (`packages/lab/data/valley/`, copied from the valley branch at `8ffe5d0`; the valley replaces them by copying new files over, then the track is re-tuned in minutes). Each slot's tool line must be **ownable**: a server of 8 owns every non-common creature line it needs with a chance of 50% or more in the week it arrives (`ownability.ts`). The tutorial boss (slot 1) is tuned for an all-common line (`SLOT_LINE`), since a week-1 server rarely owns Tempestray. Touch points are listed in `boss-track.md`.
- **Results** (the valley's roster, expected speed): a dedicated server spends one weekend a boss and reaches slots 5, 10 and 15 in weeks 5, 12 and 18; three slots miss one target narrowly (slot 7's tool worth, slots 10 and 12's classic potential just under 70%). With levels 20% behind, 23% of dedicated servers get stuck over 4 weekends, all on the third lap (slots 11, 12 and 14, Old Bogback at 14 most), and 3% with Determination on: the evidence for turning Determination on in later laps when the valley lands. Mixed servers are stuck often (information).

**How to work in the lab** (`packages/lab`, 4 cores):

- `node scripts/report.mjs` (full report, about 80 s), `--quick` (13 s), `--bosses --boss <id>` (one raid boss), `--tune [--boss <id>] [--rank all]` (fixed ranks), `--tune --track` (the track, about 3 minutes), `--path --speed all --determination` (servers week by week, seconds).
- **Agents change rules and content; the tuners set the numbers.** Targets live in `src/targets.ts`; the tests' boss rules in `src/raidRules.ts`, shared with the tuners, so a tuner cannot propose numbers a test rejects. Never loosen a test threshold to make numbers fit; a target the tuner cannot reach is a content problem.
- **Speed budget:** full report under 2 minutes, quick under 20 s, lab tests about 10 s. After merging agents' worktrees, run `pnpm worktrees:clean --yes`.
- **Settle the success measure with Misa before a tuning pass** (moving targets cost the 10-29 pass most of its time).
