# Panel 5: the balance pass against the real bosses (2026-10-29)

Reviewed: the balance pass on branch `combat-v2-bosses` (proposal v0.8; `lab-report.md`; `packages/lab/src/bosses.ts`, `bossAnswers.ts`, `dungeon.ts`). Two reviewers this time: player experience, and systems with rules (the readability and rules questions of this slice were small).

## What the reviewers found first

- **Mired is the real gate on every raid boss** (systems). Taking Squelch Song and Mud Wall out of 9 of the 10 written answers drops them to 0% to 26%; the cause is Mired's damage cut, not its slowing of Build-up. One Mire creature, from the element with no Setter or Payoff, decides the raid.
- **The prepared results only hold for the exact written line** (systems). With random legal talents the same creatures and Knacks win 0% to 88%; with no talents, 0%. Talents are meant to be about 20% of a result, not the whole weekend.
- **The boss cards point at the wrong answers** (both). Storm Sovereign's Bolt is taken by Stubborn (three times a weekend), not Night Watch; Thornmother is decided by Mud Wall and reactions more than by her armour; three second answers win without their own tool.
