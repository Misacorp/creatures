# Panel 3: the talent library, first batch (2026-10-28)

Reviewed: proposal v0.6 with R20 and R21, `modifiers.md`, and the lab's first talent batch (35 talents, per-line pools, fading Mired, the Shake It Off card, the cover cap, Cull and the killing blow). Four reviewers (rules, player experience, systems and exploits, readability), run with the `design-panel` skill.

## What the reviewers agreed on

- **The talent numbers were measured unfairly.** The report put each talent on a board chosen so its condition holds, and compared it with the same board without the talent, so the cost of standing there was hidden. Flank Guard (Left) "+18%" is -1.3% against a plain Guard in the middle; Lone Wolf "+22%" was measured in a slot that does not exist. "Holds 100%" was circular. The 1,000,000 HP lab boss makes Finisher read 0% and Slow Burn always on. (systems, player experience)
- **A third of the rolls did nothing visible** (Share the Load, Thick Skull, Light Sleeper, Long Shadow on its own, Brace, Momentum without its Knack), while conditions that hold by default (Odd One Out, Edge-walker in a row of three) were free stats. (all four)
- **Same name, different effect** between talents and shipped gear lines (Second Wind, Heavy Hand, Odd One Out, Edge-walker), and the naming direction contradicted Misa's ruling. (readability, systems)
- **Protection could reach immunity**: Long Shadow's intercept cut was capped at 100%, and cuts from different sources multiplied without a shared cap. (rules, systems)

## Questions for Misa

1. **Swapping talents.** You wanted a small Bait cost to switch talents, so players keep two creatures with different talents. A reviewer worries the cost punishes adapting to the raid's placement, which settles late and socially. Keep the cost always, or make swaps free until the raid locks (and cost Bait otherwise)?
