# Panel 6: the boss track, potential, luck, the valley roster and the gear plan (2026-10-31)

Reviewed: branch `combat-v2-roster` (proposal v0.8; `boss-track.md`, `gear-plan.md` (draft), `lab-report.md`, the roadmap's "Where we are"; everything since panel 5: the boss track, damage potential, the young-server ramp, the Thornmother wall, Determination, luck as a build (R7.1), the valley's roster and the ownability rule, the Knack usage finding). Four reviewers: rules, player experience (with `review-brief.md`), systems and exploits (ran the lab in a scratchpad), readability (with `review-brief.md`).

## What the reviewers found first

- **The early slots field creatures that cannot exist yet** (systems). The lab has no evolution gate: slot 1's line and the classic trio are stage 2 forms that evolve at level 15, and a daily player's best creature is level 11 in week 1 and 14 in week 2. With stage 1 forms, slot 1's prepared potential falls from 126% to 21% and slot 2's from 131% to 31%. The path's "one weekend per boss in weeks 1 to 4" is an artefact.
- **Mixed servers are stuck most of the time** (player experience, systems). At the expected speed 88% of mixed servers spend more than 4 weekends on one boss (48% with Determination). Tidal Colossus's second lap takes them 4.8 weekends, and even the card-less tutorial takes 3. The dials are fitted to four dedicated players and the classic trio only.
- **Arrival weeks ignore the Festival** (rules). Weeks 5, 10 and 15 are Festivals, so slot 5 is first fought in week 6, but it is tuned and checked against week 5's roster (slots 9 and 13 the same).
- **Rule holes in the luck work** (rules). The crit roll comes before the reaction is chosen; a Crit Charge is spent on Blocked hits and on hits that already crit; counters and heals behave in ways the text does not say; Mired delays Thornmother's armour falling.
- **Potential has an artefact** (systems). In potential mode the boss stays at 1 HP, so "boss below a third" (Finisher) is on for all damage past 100%.

## Questions for Misa

1. **Mixed servers.** They are the usual server, and today 88% of them spend more than 4 weekends on one boss, every creature knocked out each weekend. Should the track be tuned for them too, for example at most 3 weekends a boss for a mixed server, with the dedicated targets kept? (A) Yes, tune for both. (B) No, keep them as information and let Determination carry them. **Recommended: A.**
2. **Determination.** With it on, slow dedicated servers stuck over 4 weekends drop from 15% to 0%, and mixed ones from 88% to 48%. At 10 stacks (+50%) it makes the puzzle optional. (A) Turn it on from the second lap (slot 6), capped at +25%. (B) Keep it off. **Recommended: A.**
3. **A track strip on the raid screen:** the lap's five boss portraits, the current one lit, the beaten ones ticked, the wall marked. It is a new element on the raid screen, never on the fight screen. **Recommended: yes.**

No new principle was proposed.
