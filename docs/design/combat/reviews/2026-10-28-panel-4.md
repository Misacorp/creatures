# Panel 4: raid bosses, dungeon foes and Build-up (2026-10-28)

Reviewed: the lab's five raid bosses and fifteen dungeon foes (`packages/lab/src/bosses.ts`, `foes.ts`, `dungeon.ts`, `bossAnswers.ts`), the new attack grammar (scatter, chain, columns, rows, cross, checker; back and weakest anchors; Element Resistance), the boss Store ("Escalation", now **Build-up**) and the mechanics coverage check, against proposal v0.7 and Misa's content direction of 2026-10-28. Four reviewers: rules, player experience, systems and exploits, readability.

## What the reviewers found first

- **The best raid lines used talents their creatures cannot roll** (systems). Brace on Voltaro, Tempestray and Tidewing; Unbothered on Pyrelynx, Magmole and Ancientshell; Catch Breath on 5 of 6 carriers. With legal talents those lines fall from about 95% to 0%–28% weekend wins; a corrected search lands at 84% to 100% with varied talents. "Brace, Unbothered and Catch Breath dominate" was mostly this mistake.
- **The search cannot find good teams from scratch**, so the best lines and the usage tables leaned on the written answers (systems).
- **One Squelch Song froze every Build-up** (all four), because any Mired stack held it and Squelch keeps at least one stack up.
- **Most random attacks are never shown before they land** (rules, readability, player experience).

## Questions for Misa

1. **Random attacks.** Should every random boss attack light its target slots at round start, so players can see where it will land, or should non-wind-up random attacks stay a surprise?
2. **Dungeon difficulty.** Is about 30% to 50% of runs won at depth 5 and 15% to 30% at depth 7 (for a good all-round team) the right toughness?
3. **The camp.** Should the camp be a choice (rest, or swap a creature or Knack for the floor boss you can see ahead) instead of an automatic rest?
