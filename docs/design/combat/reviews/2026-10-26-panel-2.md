# Panel 2: Knacks and talents for every species (2026-10-26)

Reviewed: proposal v0.5 plus phase 1 slice 2 (Knacks for every remaining form, combat talents with a pool per line). Four reviewers (rules, player experience, systems and exploits, readability), run with the `design-panel` skill. After this panel the proposal is v0.6.

## What the reviewers agreed on

All four found the same top problem: **talents copied Knacks under other names** (Lasting Marks was Steady Mark word for word; Fiercely Loyal was Guard's Shadow; Lone Hunter was Territorial; Shares Its Warmth was Hearth) and flavour talents were bigger copies of role talents (Zoomies of Light Feet, Gentle Glow of Gentle Touch). The roll decided the Knack choice, numbers stacked across names (Zoom + Zoomies + Light Feet reached the dodge cap), and P-06 broke. Three found **Ohmouse with Double Mark** was a Setter and a Guard in one body, P-02's own Fail example.

## What moved in the report (before, after)

- Ohmouse in the best gauntlet lines: 6 of 14, then 1.
- All at Once: the most picked Knack, then ratio 2.2 (picked about twice as often as random).
- Bloomback: 11 of 14 best gauntlet lines, then 10. Voltaro 3, then 7. **Still the main open problem.**
- Intended answers beating the classic line: 13 of 19, then 13 of 19 (the All at Once answer now needs two Setters).
- Talents: from -1% to +18.5% on their forms, now +0% to +5% damage, with Second Wind, Light Feet and Gentle Touch also saving creatures.
- Casual roster: on average 1.5 of the 3 creatures in the best line are beyond the player's top three by level (target met).

An experiment that did not help: giving Zoom to Splashet and Wickat (an Evasion Setter for Tide and Ember) left Bloomback at 10 of 14 and raised Voltaro with Zigzag to a ratio of 7.1. Reverted. Bloomback leads because of Zigzag (Taunt, dodge and a Store on one Setter, ratio 5.4) and its own Attack, not because the other elements lack Knacks.

## Next slice

1. Zigzag and Bloomback: split Zigzag's Taunt from its Store, or cost it; look at Bloomback's Attack against the other stage 2 Payoffs.
2. Never-chosen Knacks (ratio 0): Feed the Marks, Clear Skies, Primed by Pain, Night Watch, Starlight; and near zero: Living Shelter, Hearth, Drizzle, Sunbeam, Catch the Bolts. Retune or rework.
3. Talents are now fair but small (+0% to +5%). Misa asked for stronger talents earlier; raise them together in the balance pass (phase 5), or now if he prefers.
4. Bosses and dungeon foes (slot patterns, arenas, cards), including Thick Hide's armour and wide raid rows.

## Misa's answer (2026-10-26)

1. **Talent names: shared names everywhere.** One name per effect for every species ("Sharp Claws" is Attack everywhere); species flavour lives in Knacks. Recorded in R20.3 and `decisions.md`.
2. **Golden talents at the migration** (2026-10-27): a golden talent rolls golden.
3. **Knacks and evolution** (2026-10-27): a creature knows its Knacks from the catch; evolving adds the new form's Knacks (R11).
4. **Talent pools** (2026-10-27): per creature line from one shared library, not per role (R20).
