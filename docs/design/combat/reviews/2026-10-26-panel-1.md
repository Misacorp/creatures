# Panel 1: proposal v0.3 (2026-10-26)

The first run of the `design-panel` skill. Four reviewers read the proposal (v0.3), the principles, the grammar, the generated Knack catalogue and the lab report; the systems reviewer also ran variants of the lab. The lead adopted what was clearly right into **v0.4** (proposal, lab, tests, report) in the same session.

Verdicts: **Adopt** (done in v0.4), **Ask** (a question for Misa, in the proposal's open questions), **Reject** (with a reason), **Later** (agreed, not this round).

## New in the v0.4 report

- 10 of 15 intended answers beat the classic line on their card. Not yet: Circle Round and Clear the Air (Reflective Hide), Night Watch and Keep the Heat In (Great Wave), Zoom (Swarm).
- **No Knack** is in 89 of 140 top teams: many Knacks currently cost more than they give. Next: tune the Knacks that lose to "no Knack" everywhere (Downpour 1, Night Watch 1, Clear Skies 4).
- The classic line, and its species with their best Knacks, are more than 10% behind the best gauntlet line on 14 of 14 cards. 9 forms appear across the 14 best lines; Bloomback in 11 (placeholder Knacks).
- In a casual roster, 1.7 of the 3 creatures in the best line are outside the player's top three by level.
