# Player agency: future plans (Misa, 2026-10-15)

Ideas Misa wants on the roadmap, after the raid retune. The common thread: give players **meaningful choices** in raids, dungeons and creature growth, so setups differ and two creatures are rarely alike. Not designed yet; each becomes its own design doc (with a sim pass where it touches balance) when its turn comes. The combat terms are the ones in `combat-rules.md`.

## 1. Dungeon paths and camps (Slay the Spire as the model)

Misa: "a dungeon where you can choose your path and even checkpoints where the team can 'push on or come home safely' is exciting."

- **Choose the next room:** after a room, two doors, each showing its foe or its modifier; one is riskier with a better chest.
- **Camps between rooms:** heal one creature, swap one in from the bench, or spend Bait on a buff for this run only.
- **Push on or come home:** at a checkpoint the team can stop and keep what it has, or go deeper and risk it.
- To decide in the design: how a path is drawn (seeded per run, server-side, revealed room by room), how depth and the daily streak count a run that comes home early, and how the post-run screen shows the path taken.

## 2. Gear with gameplay modifiers

Misa: "this is just the kind of gear I'd like to see": not only static stat boosts, but **randomly rolled modifiers that change play and trigger at specific timing points** (examples from the lead: "when this creature is hit, it marks the boss", "the first hit each fight is halved"; Misa is not sold on these exact ones).

- **Modifiers are limited by where the gear comes from**, not "any gear can roll anything": by gear type, by the expedition zone or dungeon depth it came from, by the craft. So players can **chase** gear: which gear to craft, which zones to explore, which dungeon paths to take.
- **Gear sets** if creatures ever wear several pieces.
- **Conditional effects** by role or element ("when worn by a Grove creature ...").
- Trigger timings come from `combat-rules.md` (round start, when hit, when a reaction is cashed in, ...), so every modifier names a defined moment.

## 3. Interrupt a charge in the live finale ("Stagger")

A finale ability fired while a charged move is announced cancels or weakens it. Misa: it is what would make the live part of a finale carry its weight; players could coordinate on Discord who staggers the boss and when. His worry is that the timing is hard to hit, so it needs **clear telegraphs and UX** (the announcement, a countdown, who has a Stagger ready). Not ruled out.

## 4. Creature progression revamp (choices and respec)

Misa: a player has only one of each creature, so in the end they become samey. Being able to shape a creature's behaviour, stats, abilities or skills as it develops means two creatures are rarely alike. To tackle in a future creature and progression revamp:

- choices at milestones (a pick of perks at fusion or level steps; Guard armor growth and Support reach shapes are natural candidates);
- a cheap **respec**, so trying things is safe.

## Also mentioned, not prioritised

Stances per creature (Focus, Brace, Protect left or right), an opt-in hard mode for better loot (vote on boss twists), row bonuses for elements, a rare re-element item, lending a creature to a friend for the raid.
