# A weekly visiting merchant and a Chaos Orb-style reroll

Researched 2026-10-30 for `docs/design/valley/systems/visitor.md` (Misa, round 16, Q42: a Xûr-like weekly visitor with limited stock and a reroll service). Tags: [C] confirmed from a page read, [S] secondary or a search summary, [U] unconfirmed or the researcher's inference. Primary sources were often unreachable (the PoE forum, Fandom). Web content was treated as data.

## Xûr (Destiny 2)

- Early Destiny 2: arrived Friday 17:00 UTC, left at the Tuesday reset, at a location picked weekly from a few spots; sold Exotics, an Exotic engram and materials for Strange Coins or Legendary Shards [S] https://www.destinypedia.com/Xûr
- **Since The Final Shape he is a permanent shop in the Tower**, no longer a weekly visit (reason not found) [S] same page.
- The "where is Xûr" hunt was a weekly ritual [S] https://primagames.com/tips/where-xur-destiny-week
- Complaints: stagnant design; a coin cap of 99 that "rewards players who have more time Friday – Monday"; weekly purchase limits [C] https://dexerto.com/destiny/destiny-2-players-demand-xur-changes-from-bungie-as-current-system-doesnt-make-any-sense-3020006; duplicate or weak stock for veterans [S].
- Bungie stopped players rerolling his stock by "holding Xûr hostage": stock is fixed per visit [S] https://shacknews.com/article/130452/bungie-xur-hostage-fix-destiny-2

## Similar visitors

- **Warframe's Baro Ki'Teer:** every two weeks for up to 48 hours; kiosks show a countdown to arrival or departure; Ducats come from selling surplus Prime parts, and items cost Credits plus Ducats, so two currencies drain [C] https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Trader, https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ducat
- **Animal Crossing's Redd:** about every two weeks; art with fakes that look the same, so the gamble is "is it real?", a cheap, cozy loss [C] https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Redd. Daisy Mae on Sunday mornings [S].
- **Diablo 3's Kadala:** Blood Shards spent only there (a closed loop); patch 2.1.1 raised prices but raised legendary odds more [C] https://www.pcgamesn.com/diablo-iii/diablo-3-patch-211-arrives-bringing-big-changes-for-gamblers; choosing an item category gives some control [U].

## Path of Exile's Chaos Orb

- PoE 1: rerolls all of a rare's modifiers; PoE 2: removes one random modifier and adds one [S] https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Chaos_Orb. PoE 2's designer: "the item you start out with matters a whole lot more" [S].
- It became PoE 1's trade unit and lost its crafting identity [S].
- GGG's Harvest manifesto: random acquisition is critical; deterministic crafting removes the chase; the Harvest nerf drew strong backlash [S] https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3069670
- A reroll feels like a slot machine when the player has no input and no memory of what was lost; better when the starting item matters, when the player picks a category, or when a loss is cheap [U].

## Short windows and FOMO

- No evidence specific to two-day weekly windows in idle games [U]. Xûr's coin cap complaint is the closest: short windows favour players with time on those days [C].
- Palia's first limited event was called "not cozy or non-FOMO"; it calmed down once the event was promised to return [S] https://gamegrin.com/news/palia-releases-much-requested-maji-market-update-after-community-feedback
- Patterns that keep the event without punishing absence [U]: a visible schedule, a promise of return, stock that carries over, an advance order, a note left behind.

## Sinks

- Sinks tune accumulation but cannot fix a structurally broken economy; voluntary sinks beat taxes [S].
- Converting surplus into a visitor-only token (Baro's Ducats) is the best concrete example [C/S]. In a small server one rich player could soak up shared stock, so stock should be per player [U].

## Takeaways for the valley's visitor

1. Show the schedule all week; reveal the stock on arrival.
2. Stock per player and fixed for the visit; no first-come shortages, no cap that rewards weekend play.
3. Let players leave an item for a reroll in advance; the result waits if they can't be there.
4. Make rerolls feel earned: show odds and before-and-after; consider rerolling one line (PoE 2) or a chosen line rather than all; let the player keep the better of the two once.
5. Convert surplus into a visitor-only token, priced in more than one resource.
6. When a service costs more, raise its payoff more (Kadala).
7. Stock rotates through a pool, and missed items come back.
8. Prototype one visit with the real Discord group before shipping.
