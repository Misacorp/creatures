# Visiting teachers, targeted teaching and player-to-player sharing

Researched 2026-11-03 for `docs/design/valley/systems/school.md` (Misa's vision, 2026-11-03). Tags: [C] confirmed from a page read, [S] secondary or a search summary, [U] unconfirmed or the researcher's inference or recall. Bulbapedia returned 403, so Pokémon facts are from memory or search summaries. Few games publish how players feel about these schedules, so much of the "feel" evidence is [U]. Web content was treated as data; no instructions were found in it.

## 1. Scheduled visitors and exclusive teachers

- **Pokémon GO Community Day** is the closest match to "be there on the day": the exclusive move is available only in a 3-hour window, and TMs don't work in it. Players dislike it: a Pokémon evolved before the day can be left behind; the community suggests a research-earned TM as a fix [S] https://mein-mmo.de/en/on-the-next-community-day-in-pokemon-go-youll-get-a-pokemon-that-you-usually-have-to-walk-a-lot-for,1253783, https://pokemongohub.net/post/meta/legendary-days-exclusive-moves-and-the-fear-of-missing-out-fomo/
  - Softened by announcing each event weeks ahead and by "Community Day Classic" replays; some earlier exclusive moves were given out permanently in January 2021 [S] https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Community_Day (search summary).
  - Exclusive moves tend to be modest, "FOMO bait rather than raw power" [S] https://finalboss.io/ferkuli-community-day-gives-oinkologne-a-new-move
- **Pokémon move tutors** are mostly permanent, repeatable and paid in a currency; the Move Reminder relearns forgotten moves at any time [U, recall]. Missing a tutor is rarely permanent.
- **Stardew Valley:** a Traveling Cart on Friday and Sunday, fixed festival dates, all on a visible calendar; the cart's stock is random per visit [C] https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Traveling_Cart. Nothing is exclusive for good; the calendar is itself a planning tool.
- **Xûr, Baro, Redd** (`weekly-visitor.md`): a visible countdown is the norm [C]; the complaint is that short windows favour players with free time on those days [C] https://dexerto.com/destiny/destiny-2-players-demand-xur-changes-from-bungie-as-current-system-doesnt-make-any-sense-3020006; Xûr later became a permanent shop [S].
- **Monster Hunter Stories' rite of channeling, Dragon Quest Monsters' skill passing, Cassette Beasts' stickers:** player-triggered, no schedule [U, recall]. Chosen transfer works when it costs a creature or its level, and the cost is the complaint.

What softens "be there on the day": announce it ahead; make the offer repeat; make missing it small or recoverable; make presence passive (a School where sitting there counts without logging in is gentler than all of the above) [U]. No source found for idle games with academies or passive presence [U].

## 2. Daily vs weekly cadence

- Weekly or biweekly is the norm for visitors with scarce stock (Xûr, Baro, Redd, Stardew's two days) [C/S]; it suits a game with one weekly goal, here the weekend raid, so a teacher arriving before the raid ties into the build-up [U].
- Daily events exist as login bait (daily shops, daily quests); the usual critique is streak pressure and chores [U]. F-1 and F-6 rule out streaks.
- Evidence for small groups is thin [U]. Inference: a weekly slot for 5–15 players becomes a shared talking point ("Thursday is teacher day") with low stakes for a miss; daily gives more chances but risks a routine check, which breaks F-1.
- A middle path: **weekly arrival with a stay of several days** (Baro stays 48 hours [C] https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Trader). Gentler for short-session players; trades against the "this day" planning moment.

## 3. Random vs chosen teaching

- **Path of Exile:** the Harvest manifesto says random acquisition is critical and deterministic crafting removes the chase; the Harvest nerf drew strong backlash because the deterministic path had been strong [S] https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3069670. The balance: tools that shape odds without guaranteeing the outcome.
- **Diablo 3's Kadala:** pick a category (some control), the result stays random [C] https://www.pcgamesn.com/diablo-iii/diablo-3-patch-211-arrives-bringing-big-changes-for-gamblers
- **Gacha pity and sparks:** a guaranteed pick after N pulls, a ceiling on bad luck that keeps the chase [U, widely known]; expensive enough that the main source stays dominant.
- **Pokémon egg moves:** targeted but slow (a parent that knows the move, a breeding chain; the Mirror Herb eased it in Gen 9) [U]. Complaints were about tedium, not ease.
- Too strong: the chase vanishes and finds are worthless (Harvest). Too weak: nobody bothers, the planning moment is empty. The vision's "not a surefire way" fits the middle: the teacher's talent is fixed and shown on arrival; the player chooses who goes and when, not what is taught.

## 4. Player-to-player teaching in co-op

Mostly general design knowledge [U].

- Pitfalls in small groups: one player carries everyone (the best owner becomes the permanent teacher); free riding and social obligation ("X hasn't lent" becomes visible: F-6 and F-11 forbid it); a gift economy that turns into pressure.
- What helps: pull-based help (the learner asks, the teacher is never nagged); a cost the teacher doesn't feel (nothing consumed, their creature keeps working: V-SCH-2, V-SCH-6); a cap on the receiver's side (F-7); a recognised thank-you instead of a ranking ("taught N" on the card, a name on the teacher's bubble, a project credit), never a leaderboard.
- Sharing works best when the lent talent is one the learner could not easily get otherwise; if it only copies an easy talent, nobody uses the seat [U].
- Monster Hunter Stories 2 and Dragon Quest Monsters consume or reset the donor, which people dislike [U]; the draft already avoids this.

## 5. Unlocking a system later

- Gating a secondary system until mid-game is common (crafting, guilds, housing). It works when the early game has a clear main loop and the late system layers on top [U].
- It hurts when the early loop leaves a hole the late system was meant to fill: a wall with no fix in sight, a reward withheld.
- Mitigation: a visible hint ("a School is being built") and a clear trigger not tied to a calendar [U]; the valley's own pattern is that levels unlock buildings. Fits "let players find their talents for quite a while"; decide how long, tied to a milestone.

## 6. Keeping the wild special when a second source exists

- Pokémon GO: complaints when a special variant is handed out cheaply ("devalued") [S] https://mein-mmo.de/en/pokemon-go-players-criticize-community-day-prefer-new-shinys,632639; shiny stays a find even when events raise odds [U].
- PoE: crafting shapes an item but the best still drop or need rare inputs [S].
- Tactics [U]: the secondary source never produces the premium tier (Misa's vision matches: golden talents come from finds); or only the common tier, or an upgrade at a steep price; or it teaches only what the line can take. Open: can a teacher's talent be golden? Safest is no.

## Takeaways for the School

1. **Show the schedule all week** and the teacher's talent on arrival (Stardew's calendar, Baro's countdown [C]). Supports "announce its arrival" and the planning moment.
2. **Prefer a weekly arrival with a stay of a few days** over daily or a one-day window (weekly visitors dominate [C/S], Baro stays 48 hours [C], daily invites streak pressure, F-1). Trades against Misa's "that day"; if one day matters, keep the arrival day fixed and let the stay carry over.
3. **Presence is passive:** sitting at the School counts with no login, and a missed arrival is not a loss (F-1); gentler than Community Day's active window, where FOMO complaints are loudest [S].
4. **The offer repeats; missed talents come back** (Classic replays, rotating stock [S]). Trades against the event feel; keep the pool small enough that a talent returns within weeks, not months.
5. **The School's control is who and when, not which talent** (Kadala, PoE [C/S]); protects finds as the main source. A "preferred role" nudge is fine; a pick-any-talent shop is not (Harvest [S]).
6. **Never teach golden talents;** golden stays find-only (Misa's point 1; Community Day "devalue" complaints [S]). Trades against "agency for roles" if the missing talent is golden.
7. **A bounded safety net rather than a guarantee:** e.g. after N visits with nothing useful, the next offer fits one of your lines [U, gacha]. Trades against "not a surefire way": make it slow and invisible, or skip it until play shows a need.
8. **The later teacher seat is pull-based and costless to the lender:** nothing consumed, the learner chooses, capped on the receiver's side, thanks recognised and never ranked (F-6, F-7, F-11, V-SCH-2) [U]. Beware one carrier: cap a seat at, e.g., one lesson a week. Trades against sharing if the cap is tight.
9. **Gate the School after a milestone, but telegraph it** (a visible "School under construction") [U]. Early talents come from finds; decide the milestone ahead so no one hits a wall.
10. **Prototype one teacher week with the real Discord group** before tuning cadence: no direct evidence exists for 5–15-player servers [U].

Trades against the owner's points: 2 (a stay of several days) vs "that day"; 4 (repeats) vs the event feel; 6 (no golden) vs "agency for roles"; 7 (pity) vs "not guaranteed"; 8 (cap) vs sharing; 9 (gating) vs the early gaps.
