## Other friction

- **Force push denied** (10-25 19:04) when restarting the branch after the DMs squash merge; worked around with a merge commit that keeps the old tip. The stop hook then nagged four times about unpushed commits after branch resets and merges.
- **A parallel agent started from `main`, not the session branch** (the data prune), so migration numbers had to be coordinated by message and its seams fixed at merge (a removed test option, the away test that assumed auto-joining).
- **`docs/design/decisions.md` conflicted on every merge** (four times): every agent appends its entry at the end.
- **Contract fixtures drifted on `main` unnoticed.** #87's lounger change moved 21 captured fixtures, and #73 had hand-typed dungeon numbers into one; a subagent then hand-edited 37 more. `pnpm fixtures:capture --check` is not part of `pnpm check` or CI, so nothing failed.
- **Two shortcuts found by the probe were already noted as "left as is"** in earlier decisions (the start-up passes, the live finale scan), with a cost estimate that ignored how often they run.

## What went well

- **Measure first.** A small probe that summed `cursor.rowsRead` per statement found every cause, and it became the read budget test (with a growth check) and the `db.rows_read` metric in the daily report.
- **Parallel worktree agents** with tight briefs built six pieces overnight; each was reviewed and merged by the lead, with all checks green on every PR.
- **Misa's principles** (keep stats, delete details; join by acting; push, don't poll) turned single fixes into rules, now in `decisions.md` ("What the rows_read week taught us").
